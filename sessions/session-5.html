Session 5

Session Summary

In-Game Date: April 11, 2022
Dimension: 24

Key Events

  • Street battle continues: Automatic Man blasts The Master, briefly staggering him; Khaki Tacs fire with no effect. An RPG aimed toward Automatic Man detonates where he stood moments before.
  • Fire Cube manipulated: A beam from The Master links to Fire Cube, who erupts in rage and hurls a massive fireball that detonates near Automatic Man, triggering widespread damage and car alarms. Automatic Man counters and drops Fire Cube.
  • Van explosion: The Handsy Plumber van drives off and then explodes in the street. The blast throws Mantis Shrimp onto a car, cracks his shell, and shakes the area.
  • Tunnel clash: M.O.L.E.S. withdraw toward the tunnel, re-forming their darkness. Automatic Man wrestles one MOLE unconscious; Mantis shrinks and is carried deeper before enlarging and ending up with The Master in the tunnel.
  • Case is empty: Templar reaches the weapons case—it’s empty (contents detonated earlier).
  • Barbara’s account: Barbara identifies herself as Barbara Wells and describes captivity “in the Underworld,” her roommate Jennifer being eaten by MOLES, and The Master’s vow not to read her mind. She says The Master works with the government and can implant false memories; she witnessed a woman later used in a public accusation (Eric Bunch case). She has been underground ~6 months and urges moving before MOLES return.
  • Regroup at hotel: Party relocates to the Bossanova Hotel. Staff greet Automatic Man as “Mr. VonNewman,” provide a penthouse key and a package: Ian VonNewman driver’s license, keys, valet kit, small spiral notebook of numbers, satellite phone, a KC Star (last Wednesday), and an address near Mimi’s Pantry in Riverside. Two Men-in-Black types linger in the lobby.
  • Signals and tunnels: Automatic Man traces the control signal to the municipal airport. Barbara notes tunnels run everywhere under the city and an entrance by the river. She says The Master wanted revenge on The Gnome and has a recurring dream of someone in a forest closing in on him.

Heroes

  • Automatic Man — Drops Fire Cube, confirms airport-based control signal, recognized as “Ian VonNewman” at the hotel, receives a package with identity items and intel.
  • Mantis Shrimp — Carries beeping device, is blown by the van’s explosion, fights MOLES in and near the tunnel, confronts The Master in darkness.
  • Templar — Attempts shots during the melee, reaches the case (empty), later coordinates with the team.
  • Larry Harry Jones — Ego attack topples The Master temporarily; protects and questions Barbara; coordinates the move to the hotel.

Villains and Threats

  • The Master — Telepath who manipulates Fire Cube, taunts heroes, and escapes MOLE shrouds; tied to government work and memory implantation.
  • M.O.L.E.S. — Retreat and regroup in darkness; responsible for abductions and killings underground.
  • Fire Cube — Arrives in fury, severely escalates the fight, then is blasted down by Automatic Man.

Other Characters

  • Barbara Wells — Former captive; key eyewitness to The Master, MOLES’ atrocities, tunnels, and a river entrance; pushes to kill The Master.
  • Khaki Tacs — Engage with small arms and explosives; begin packing up to leave post-blast.
  • Audi driver — 911 call, flees amid chaos.
  • Bossanova Hotel staff — Welcome Automatic Man as “Mr. VonNewman” and deliver the package.

Notes

  • Barbara claims The Master is registered with the government and implants memories; she cites an incident involving a woman and a later accusation against Councilman Eric Bunch.
  • Barbara names Kevin McMannis (councilman) as trapped underground.
  • Plot leads: municipal airport transmitter; address near Mimi’s Pantry (Riverside); river tunnel entrance; The Master’s forest dream; The Master’s vendetta against The Gnome.
