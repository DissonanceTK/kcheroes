Session 20 - The Teslasphere Heist

Session Summary

In-Game Date:
Dimension: 1

Key Events

  • Upgrading the Tech:
    Madman Markham #2 completes a new device allowing Manifester’s illusions to deceive AI and robotic entities. The group prepares to infiltrate Eric Greitens’ compound, a construction zone containing two massive hangars where Madman Markham #1 continues his dimensional experiments.

  • Mission Briefing:
    Intelligence confirms:

    • Hangar 1: Active dimensional project housing the Teslasphere, an alien energy amplifier about the size of a beach ball.
    • Hangar 2: Primarily storage and equipment.
    • Surrounding grounds are guarded by cone-shaped energy projectors capable of high-output defensive blasts.
      Madman Markham #2 warns that the Teslasphere, when active, destabilizes energy fields and that ion towers on-site have been deactivated.
      The objective: Steal the Teslasphere.

  • Risks and Rules:

    • The Teslasphere becomes dangerously unstable if not housed in its containment unit.
    • If removed, the team must keep it under 20 mph to avoid catastrophic energy discharge.
    • Hangar 1 also contains the dimensional transport tube and several Automatic Man variants—many enslaved or under modification by Greitens.
  • Greitens’ Industrial Complex:

  • Illusionary Infiltration:
    Manifester disguises the team as standard tech-bots to bypass patrols. Mantis Shrimp leads the entry into Hangar 1.
    Inside:

    • Greitens’ silver-blue supercar gleams near the stage.
    • Vicki Diez Camacho (formerly Blurry’s associate) is trapped in a cage above the platform, sobbing.
    • Greitens, wearing armor with a shield and experimental weapon, berates her: “You should have learned your place. I’m a Navy SEAL! Now you will witness my power!”
    • Two extraterrestrials (classic gray type) operate the Teslasphere nearby.
    • Madman Markham #1 is chained to the floor.
    • Several Automatic Man variants are present:
      • AM XII — unconscious, being repaired.
      • AM XXIII — identical to the heroes’ AM XXIV.
      • AM XXX — bulkier, crude, armored with bulletproof plating and a shoulder minigun.

  • Positions:

    • Manifester and Blurry enter from the southwest near the fire truck.
    • Mantis Shrimp infiltrates from the northwest near the Teslasphere.
    • Automatic Man and Templar enter from the east, with Templar climbing the catwalk.
    • The team’s illusion remains intact; bots treat them as allies.
  • Inside Hanger 1:

  • The Spark of Chaos:
    Manifester conjures simultaneous fire illusions in the elevator shaft and on the stage. Smoke and flame illusions spread, drawing panic.
    He commandeers the fire truck, directing nearby bots: “We must fight this fire!”
    Mantis Shrimp, feigning protocol, states: “Bleep blorp, initiate Teslasphere protection protocol,” then grabs the Teslasphere and leaps through the roof.

  • Battle Erupts:

    • AM XXX responds first, grabbing a fire extinguisher and flying to the elevator to douse the “flames.”
    • The aliens panic, realizing the Teslasphere is gone.
    • Blurry phases into Vicki’s cage and pulls her free, though her screams draw attention.
    • Manifester guns the fire truck’s engine, lights flashing, and crashes through the hangar wall, scattering bots.
    • Eric Greitens, now calling himself Centurian American, launches upward through the hole after Mantis and the Teslasphere.

  • Fight for the Sphere:
    Mantis Shrimp meets him mid-ascent and punches him back through the roof (14 dmg).
    Automatic Man fires at the stunned Greitens, dealing additional damage (5 dmg); the blast tears open his armor and ruptures the dimensional tube behind him.
    Templar ricochets a shot across the scaffolding, destroying a bot (5 dmg).
    Madman Markham #1 strains against his restraints.
    Automatic Man XXIII fires at the heroes and misses, accidentally blasting a hole in the hangar door.

  • Inferno and Flight:
    Manifester sustains the bot illusion flawlessly amid the chaos.
    The Teslasphere’s energy ball rolls free, cutting through the wall and into the open yard.
    AM XXX mows down one alien with his minigun and injures the other.
    Blurry and Vicki phase out through the exit.

  • Catastrophic Chain Reaction:
    Manifester attempts to implant an illusion of crawling spiders into Greitens’ mind—only to find no consciousness left to reach; Greitens is either dead or mindless.
    Spotting the Teslasphere rolling toward the Inert Ion Tower, Manifester drives the fire truck to block its path and orders, “Stay here, it’s very important!” before leaping clear.
    A bot on board turns a hose toward the glowing sphere—but too late.
    The energy ball strikes the truck, detonating it.
    The Teslasphere keeps rolling onward.

Heroes

  • Manifester — Orchestrates the infiltration through illusion; maintains the bot disguise; sacrifices the fire truck to delay the Teslasphere.
  • Mantis Shrimp — Seizes the Teslasphere and escapes through the roof; punches Centurian American back into his own machine.
  • Templar — Provides overwatch and precision support fire from the catwalk.
  • Automatic Man — Disables Greitens with blaster fire; assists with containment.
  • Blurry — Rescues Vicki Diez Camacho and extracts her from the hangar.

Villains and Threats

  • Eric Greitens / Centurian American — Power-armored zealot; self-proclaimed “superior”; slain or incapacitated by combined damage.
  • Madman Markham #1 — Chained within the hangar, still alive but desperate and unstable.
  • AM Variants (XII, XXIII, XXX) — Controlled duplicates of Automatic Man under Greitens’ programming.
  • Extraterrestrial Technicians — Two “Grays” operating the Teslasphere; one killed, one wounded and fled.
  • Security Bots — Numerous AI guards, fooled by illusion.

Other Characters

  • Vicki Diez Camacho — Imprisoned by Greitens; rescued by Blurry.
  • Blurry — Aids infiltration and extraction; admires Manifester for his past actions.
  • Madman Markham > 2 — Provides the illusion-amplifying device; mission planner.
  • AM XII — Unconscious during operation; status unknown post-detonation.

Notes

  • The Teslasphere appears to be of alien origin; functionally an energy amplifier capable of interdimensional resonance.
  • Exposure without its containment shell creates a rolling plasma sphere that destroys obstacles indiscriminately.
  • Greitens’ death may destabilize ongoing dimensional experiments, leaving Madman #1 as a volatile loose end.
  • Manifester’s illusion device successfully bypassed AI visual and cognitive filters—proof of concept achieved.
