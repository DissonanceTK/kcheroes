Session 3

Session Summary

In-Game Date: April 11, 2022
Dimension: 24

Key Events

  • Weapons Exchange Turns Violent:
    Continuing from the prior session’s standoff, chaos erupts at the parking garage near 9th and Wyandotte. Mantis Shrimp resizes just as a BMW speeds toward the weapons dealers, launching the car over their heads and causing it to crash.

  • The Arrival of Trenchcoat:
    A mysterious figure in a trench coat exits the Handsy Plumber van and attacks with energy blasts, disabling several Khaki Techs (federal-style agents).

    • [Paul says:] identifies them as Khaki Tacs — shorthand for “Khaki Tactics,” a term adopted by the players.

  • Escalation and Illusions:
    Manifester teleports into action, saving a wounded man and deploying illusions to distract the attackers. Templar joins by closing distance through cover and physical assault.
    Mantis Shrimp uses his accordion as a blunt weapon to incapacitate one gunman “Looney Tunes style.”

  • Supernatural Interference:
    A black, oscillating cloud rises from the ground—revealed as the presence of the M.O.L.E.S., strange entities tied to underground tunnels.
    Voices echo telepathically:

    • “You simple creatures. Don’t eat him. Just get the bag.”
      [Paul says:] Only Mantis Shrimp perceives the voice directly and sees color distortions invisible to normal humans.

  • Introduction of the Ashen Figure:
    A pale, large-headed being with purple openings in its skin appears near the tunnel entrance, identified as the Leader of the M.O.L.E.S. or a related entity.

    • [Paul says:] It emits a non-visible spectrum beam that spreads across the scene before retreating into the tunnel.

  • Trenchcoat’s Power Display:
    Trenchcoat shows resilience to Templar’s chokehold and Manifester’s psychic and illusion attacks. Physical strikes and bullets have minimal effect.

  • Retreat into the Tunnel:
    The M.O.L.E.S. retrieve the duffle bag and withdraw underground. Mantis Shrimp pursues them into the darkness while Templar regroups in the parking garage.
    Manifester confronts Trenchcoat, stunning him with a psychic assault before the mysterious figure loads an injured man into the van.

  • Scene Ends:
    Combat pauses with heroes divided—Mantis in the tunnel, Templar ascending the parking garage, and Manifester standing near Trenchcoat and the van.

Heroes

  • Mantis Shrimp – Uses size manipulation, hand-to-hand combat, and improvised weapons; witnesses and interacts with the M.O.L.E.S. directly.
  • Templar – Provides tactical strikes and support, engages Trenchcoat physically, and later retreats to regroup.
  • Larry Harry Jones – Uses teleportation, psychic powers, and illusions to protect civilians and disorient enemies.

Villains and Threats

  • Trenchcoat (Automatic Man) – A mysterious combatant using energy projection and extreme durability.
  • Khaki Techs / “Khaki Tacs” – Human operatives, likely federal or paramilitary, heavily armed.
  • M.O.L.E.S. – Blobby, semi-corporeal dark entities operating from an underground tunnel network.
  • Ashen-Skinned Figure – Possibly the leader or controller of the M.O.L.E.S.; displays energy-based and psychic powers.

Other Characters

  • BMW Driver / Gunman – Participant in the arms exchange, incapacitated early in the fight.
  • Duffle Bag Holder – Mortally wounded; bag containing unknown device or data stolen by the M.O.L.E.S.
  • Civilian Bystanders – Present in nearby areas; at least one is rescued by Manifester.

Notes

This session features the first full-scale multi-faction conflict of the campaign: heroes, agents, M.O.L.E.S., and Trenchcoat’s unknown agenda collide.
It also introduces psychic phenomena and non-visible energy spectra, expanding the world’s definition of “anomalies.”
The name “Khaki Tacs,” coined by Paul, becomes semi-canonical in later sessions.
