Session 14 - 714379

Session Summary

It’s a warm, sunny Saturday — the day of the Inter-Dojo Tournament.

Since the last events:

  • Josh Hawley was reportedly seen in two places at once (Washington, D.C. and the fight in K.C.).
  • Eric Bunch is scheduled for a bond modification hearing.
  • Street rumors suggest Meat Shield is hiring assassins to kill one or more of the heroes.

But today belongs to martial arts glory.

Tournament Setup

The Next Up Challenge is being held at Builder’s Steel Facility, thanks to Jimbo Shrump, who secured the space with permission from Mark Renallo.
Hosted by Big Bingo, a tall, portly man in a white suit with bingo numbers printed across it.
Catering provided by Captain Happy’s Catfish House — the fish blimp is floating above the event.

Prize: a ceremonial katana and a briefcase of cash.

Events:

  • Kata demonstrations (on a flatbed truck)
  • Pugil stick battle atop a steel girder
  • “Next Up” challenger bracket

Competitors:

  • Mantis Shrimp vs. Hipster Deadpool
  • Crane kick (Karate Kid crane style)
  • Nunchucks (duplicates himself)
  • Giraffe (stretchy neck, headbutts)
  • Blue Ninja (mysterious, skilled, later revealed as Ghost Dancer)

Spectators include anomalies Rainbow (colorful bodysuit, bo staff) and The Union (rugby-themed team: 8 Man, Scrum Half, and Chubby Chaser #2).

Event Begins

Big Bingo hypes the crowd. Templar senses something off (fails Perception).
Hipster Deadpool opens with an incredible display of parkour and acrobatics.
Mantis Shrimp follows with a baffling mix of mime and interpretive dance.
Meanwhile, Manifester enjoys catfish tacos.

When they climb the girder, Hipster Deadpool moves with poise; Mantis hits his head on the beam with his jetpack.

ActiVADER Arrives

A black Doritos truck blaring the Imperial March pulls up.
Six people in stormtrooper outfits and a tall “Darth Vader” impersonator emerge — ActiVADER, a delusional anomaly who alters others’ powers.
He gestures toward the katana as if to telekinetically pull it to himself, but nothing happens.

Initiative: Templar, Magister, Mantis Shrimp

Hipster Deadpool takes advantage of the distraction to cheap-shot Mantis for 6 damage.

Templar retrieves his hidden gear. Manifester changes into costume.
Mantis slams his tail into the girder, knocking it down 40 feet.
Hipster Deadpool falls beneath it — crushed to death — but immediately begins regenerating.

Chaos Unleashed

The Rugby Boys dash for the money.
Templar fires a ricochet shot, dropping most stormtroopers and injuring ActiVADER.
ActiVADER attacks Manifester, who opens a portal high above but doesn’t fall through.
Mantis grabs a stormtrooper and throws him across the field, bouncing him into Jimbo Shrump and Big Bingo’s table.

Manifester attempts a mind blast on ActiVADER but almost hits Mantis.
A police helicopter approaches, announcing: “Unlawful anomaly assembly. Disperse or be arrested.”
A Boba Fett-costumed figure exits a van and shoots down the helicopter — it spirals toward the fish blimp.

Nunchucks splits in half and charges Big Bingo.
Templar notices Blue Ninja moves with tactical precision — reminding him of someone terrifying from their home dimension.
Giraffe stretches his neck to try and grab the blimp.
Cranekick hops and poses dramatically to “You’re the Best Around.”

Battle Escalates

ActiVADER attacks Mantis, disrupting his powers — Mantis partially transforms back into Claude Crevette.
Blue Ninja goes invisible; Mantis realizes it’s Ghost Dancer.
Templar shoots ActiVADER again, wounding him heavily.

Manifester creates an illusion that ActiVADER is falling from the portal above — it works perfectly.
Boba Fett rockets up, grabs ActiVADER, and they blast into the sky.
Ghost Dancer approaches Mantis.

Mantis uses his ion pack to catch the crashing helicopter, redirecting it safely into a bounce house, saving civilians — but injuring himself (4 dmg).
Templar shoots Boba Fett’s jetpack, sending him spinning and crashing behind a building.

Manifester tries to make the Rugby Boys think the briefcase is full of rats — fails spectacularly.
Everyone except the Rugby Union believes the illusion; Big Bingo freaks out.
Jimbo Shrump tries to stab imaginary rats with the katana, allowing the Rugby Boys to grab the cash and bolt.

Ghost Dancer swings at Mantis and misses.
Mantis uppercuts him while shrunk, dealing 12 damage; Ghost Dancer becomes visible and falls unconscious.

Big Bingo, regaining his composure, gets back on the mic:

“This is the best inter-dojo tournament north or south of the river!”

Mantis helps a regenerated Hipster Deadpool to his feet; both play it off like part of the show.

Encounter with The Fiddler

Behind the facility, Templar, Manifester, and Mantis Shrimp investigate where ActiVADER and Boba Fett crashed.
They find both unconscious — and are confronted by a man with a gun.

It’s Madman Markham, also known as The Fiddler.

Templar: “Stay on the ground.”
Markham: “Templar, is that you?”
Templar: “Yes.”
Markham: “I am not the Madman that you seek.”
