Session 11 - West Bottoms

Session Summary

In-Game Date: April 11, 2022
Dimension: 1

Key Events

  • Templar vs. Windy:
    Templar continues to swirl within the storm, firing at the armored wind-controller (dubbed Windy). His shot lands true, slamming Windy into the ground and breaking the cyclone apart.
    He throws a rappel line onto a nearby building and swings toward the collapsing battle zone.

  • Manifester’s Attempt:
    Manifester tries to create an illusion of Automatic Man’s Celica falling onto Windy, but the illusion fails.

  • Windy’s Escape:
    Windy uses gusts of air to hurl himself into a second-story window of a nearby building.

  • Mantis Shrimp’s Legal Interlude:
    Mantis Shrimp’s cell phone rings—his lawyer Paul Hood is calling about his ongoing dispute with A.R.I.E.S..
    Paul also mentions that “Deadpool” (possibly a codename or alias) is looking for Mantis—and he’s riding a Vespa.

  • Tourists Arrive:
    A loudspeaker blares from a passing Mark Sargent Reality Tour bus:

    “The government thinks they can keep lying to us! First they said the Earth is round—now they say superheroes are real!”
    Tourists see Mantis Shrimp and exclaim he’s real; Mark insists he’s “an animatronic.”
    The bus later flees after witnessing him catch a falling car.

  • Catching the Celica:
    The crane’s motor fails—the Celica plummets.
    Mantis leaps beneath it, catching the falling car in a dramatic show of strength.
    Passersby cheer as the bus speeds off in panic.
    Mantis gently places Automatic Man into the car, covers him with a jacket, and then jetpacks toward the building Windy entered.

Combat in the Buildings

  • Templar’s Ascent:
    Templar leaps from a burnt-out car onto an awning and heads for the second floor, where he hears a woman calling for help.

  • Manifester’s Encounter:
    Inside, Manifester finds a woman in a harness chained to a pillar.
    When she spots him, she turns invisible, though the chain and harness remain visible.
    Manifester quips,

    “Looks like I’m interrupting something… I didn’t realize that club had moved here.”
    The chain retracts around the pillar—its end unseen.

  • Bulltoad Appears:
    From the shadows emerges a mutant hybrid of human, toad, and bull, who bellows,

    “You shouldn’t have come here. Now Bulltoad’s gonna have to kick your ass!”
    Manifester psychically blasts him, but Bulltoad retaliates—goring Manifester and hurling him through a window onto an awning.

    “Carmelita, you know I only keep you chained up because of certain masculine insecurities,” Bulltoad mutters.

  • Templar’s Counterattack:
    Templar shoots Bulltoad mid-rant, instantly knocking him out, splattering goo across the room—and across Manifester.
    “You killed him! You killed him, you horrible monster!” Carmelita screams, still restrained.

  • The Invisible Threat and Concrete Arrival:
    Mantis rockets up toward the roof in search of Windy but instead encounters a new foe made of living concrete. The invisible woman (Carmelita) fires energy blasts at Templar and misses. Manifester tries a psychic counterattack, but also misses.
    Concrete bellows,

    “You’ll have to do better than that!”
    He slams the floor, collapsing it. Templar and Mantis both plummet to the lower level.
    Concrete then melds into the ceiling to pursue them.

  • The Climactic Blow:
    Mantis shrinks down, then suddenly resizes mid-punch, uppercutting the Concrete villain straight through the ceiling.
    The sound of collapsing masonry echoes moments later.

  • Aftermath and Escape:
    The heroes stabilize Bulltoad (still alive) and free Carmelita, leaving her restrained but safe.
    They prepare to exit the No-Go Zone, where Khaki Tacs are stationed at the perimeter.
    Manifester disguises their vehicles as Khaki Tac transports using illusion.
    They successfully slip past security undetected.

    In the Celica, the magnetized CD player now plays “Give It Up” by KC & The Sunshine Band.
    Inside the glovebox, they find a fancy costume catalogue addressed to a mansion on Ward Parkway—their next lead.

Heroes

  • Templar: Marksman and acrobat; eliminates Bulltoad and leads tactical movement.
  • Larry Harry Jones: Illusionist and psychic; discovers Carmelita, engages Bulltoad, and later facilitates escape.
  • Mantis Shrimp: Strength and agility; rescues Automatic Man and defeats Concrete in a cinematic knockout.
  • Automatic Man: Unconscious for most of the encounter, but rescued from the Celica wreck.

Supporting Cast

  • Carmelita: Invisible woman, formerly chained captive of Bulltoad.
  • Bulltoad: Mutant villain (bull/toad hybrid); defeated by Templar.
  • Windy: Armored wind-controller; escaped early in the fight.
  • Concrete: Living stone powerhouse; smashed through ceiling by Mantis Shrimp.
  • Paul Hood: Mantis Shrimp’s persistent and possibly overworked lawyer.
  • Mark Sargent: Flat Earth tour guide; witnesses Mantis’s heroism but remains unconvinced.

Fun & Poignant Moments

  • The “Mark Sargent Reality Tours” scene—perfect Kansas City absurdity meets meta-comedy.
  • Mantis catching the Celica mid-fall—a classic comic-book splash page moment.
  • Manifester’s line to the invisible woman: “Didn’t realize that club moved here.”
  • The CD player humor—Mantis’s Celica now permanently tied to Kansas City’s disco heritage.
