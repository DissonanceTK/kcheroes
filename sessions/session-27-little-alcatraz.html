Session 27 - Little Alcatraz

Session Summary

In-Game Date: Fall, 1949
Dimension: 45

Key Events

  • New Threats North of the River:
    After the carnival, Manifester, Mantis Shrimp, and Dissonance return home. Late that night, sirens echo near the river. By morning, Manifester receives a visit from Al Capone and one of his muscle. Capone reports that Japanese supervillains attacked a northern detention camp for Japanese Americans. Ever the opportunist, Capone proposes converting one of his barges into a makeshift prison—“my own little Alcatraz”—and offers the heroes $50 a day to act as affiliated “security consultants.”
    Manifester agrees, and Capone advances them $250, promising federal approval through Boss Tom.

  • Echoes and Intelligence:
    Automatic Man calls via radio, confirming the attack. Using his Ye Olde Floppy Disc of Strategic Insight, he detects coded transmissions hidden in Kansas City Rose’s broadcasts, suggesting deeper Axis coordination. The chatter around the detention center is abnormally heavy, centered on a Japanese detainee called Nico.

  • Dissonance’s Duality:
    Dissonance (Thomas Black) tells Mantis of his fragmented memories—visions, a voice “more beautiful than any I’ve heard,” and a sense of not needing to eat anymore. His voice sometimes splits, echoing with two tones at once.

  • Town Topic Debate:
    Over hamburgers, Manifester pays Mantis and Dissonance their shares. The team debates Capone’s offer and the morality of detaining citizens. Mantis argues for freeing the internees; Manifester urges caution. When a bigot comments about “no good Japs,” Mantis punches him, and the group exits quickly.

  • The “Cat” Named Nico:
    Mantis makes contact with his French resistance network via code phrase “Johnny has a long mustache.” They reveal that Japanese agents are targeting a detainee named Nico (“cat”)—possibly the Emperor’s grandson who defied imperial orders. Three Japanese supers were dispatched to retrieve him:

    • Ōzeki — a sumo champion who can grow to immense size.
    • Lame de l'Empereur (Emperor’s Blade) — a samurai with dimension-cutting katana.
    • Nehan — an airborne energy-blaster monk in white and red robes.

  • Reconnaissance of “Little Alcatraz”:
    On the river, the heroes find Capone’s barge operation—Japanese-American prisoners chained beneath a canopy while mafiosos wire red gasoline barrels along the deck. Manifester deduces it’s rigged for detonation in case of attack. They decide to wait for nightfall.

  • New Reports from AM:
    Automatic Man reports that liberated Japanese detainees were later found decapitated—punished for refusing allegiance. “Nico” remains captive; chatter suggests the Emperor himself demands his retrieval.

  • Night Assault on the River:
    Under cover of darkness, the heroes approach the barge (driver: Sebastian). It now floats midstream with only two bots maintaining it. Manifester positions himself on the rail bridge; Mantis infiltrates the tugboat, signaling “The crustacean is in the pot.” Dissonance hovers above as overwatch.

  • Combat: The Assault on Little Alcatraz

    • Opening Gambit:
      Manifester creates the illusion of a massive ship behind them, with Dissonance amplifying its horn blast. Mantis yanks the anchor; the tug surges forward.

    • Ōzeki’s Entrance:
      The bridge groans. A giant man leaps from it—Ōzeki—slamming onto the barge, pitching Dissonance into the water. The deck quakes.
      Dissonance regains flight; Manifester opens a portal at the barge’s edge.

    • Nehan Descends:
      A glowing woman in white and red robes approaches from the west, radiant energy flaring.

    • Engagement:
      Mantis taunts Ōzeki by juggling a gasoline barrel. Mafiosos open fire, grazing the sumo. Ōzeki rips the prisoners’ chains free and hurls them overboard, muttering about “suitable deaths for traitors.”

    Dissonance retaliates with a resonant blast, staggering Ōzeki (10 dmg).
    Nehan fires beams of radiant light—Cadillacs explode, and the mob flees.

    Manifester attempts a blindness illusion on Nehan—partial success.

    • The Emperor’s Blade Appears:
      A shimmering rift opens; a samurai steps through, slicing the chain mid-fall: “Time to die, Nico.” He readies his katana for execution.

    • Countermoves:
      Dissonance lifts the prisoners clear of the water.
      Mantis shrinks mid-grapple, flipping Ōzeki into the river, then catches the gasoline barrel in one hand.
      Manifester’s illusion fails; Emperor’s Blade remains focused.
      Dissonance interposes: “He’s under protection of the United States.”
      Blade: “Then the United States will fall with him.” (Hits Dissonance for 8 dmg.)

    Nehan’s blind firing misses everyone; steam rises off the river as her power flares.
    Ōzeki claws at the tugboat, threatening to overturn it.

    • Turning the Tide:
      Manifester’s next illusion—swarm of stinging bees—connects, damaging the samurai (6 dmg).
      The Emperor’s Blade shimmers and vanishes, commanding, “Nehan, kill them all!”
      Nehan’s light intensifies; Mantis hauls the prisoners through Manifester’s portal as he yells, “Quick—get in!”
      Dissonance delivers one last blast before diving through. Manifester seals the portal.

    • Aftermath:
      The barge and tug detonate in a blinding chain explosion. A wave surges down the river; Ōzeki is hurled into the current.
      Manifester’s illusion convinces all observers—including the Japanese supers—that the prisoners perished in the blast.
      Dissonance silences the noise of the rescue, ensuring secrecy.

Heroes

  • Manifester — Negotiates Capone’s deal, coordinates illusions in battle, rescues prisoners via portal, maintains cover through deception.
  • Mantis Shrimp — Infiltration expert; flips Ōzeki into the river while juggling live ordnance; extracts prisoners through portal.
  • Blurry — Off-screen for most events, focusing on training and strategy with Capone; likely to rejoin next session.
  • Dissonance — Provides sonic and harmonic assaults; protects detainees; silences evidence of their rescue.
  • Automatic Man — Off-site intelligence support; decodes Kansas City Rose’s transmissions.

Villains and Threats

  • Ōzeki — Giant sumo, strength-type; thrown into the river by Mantis.
  • Nehan — Flying energy blaster; partially blinded, retreats after explosion.
  • Lame de l’Empereur (Emperor’s Blade) — Samurai with dimension blade; vanishes mid-fight, vows revenge.
  • The Japanese Empire — Actively operating superhuman agents within U.S. borders.

Other Characters

  • Al Capone — Financier of the “Little Alcatraz” project; profits from the chaos under guise of patriotism.
  • Tom Pendergast — Facilitator for Capone’s government connections.
  • Automatic Man — Remote analyst tracking coded broadcasts.
  • Nico — Japanese-American detainee, grandson of the Emperor; rescued by heroes, presumed dead by enemies.
  • Sebastian — Capone’s driver.
  • Japanese-American Prisoners — Civilians saved from the barge, now hidden.

Notes

  • Moral Dilemma: Heroes’ involvement with Capone creates tension between profit and principle.
  • Coded Broadcasts: Kansas City Rose’s music doubles as encrypted Axis communication.
  • Capone’s Alcatraz: Symbolic of fascist paranoia bleeding into homefront America.
  • Illusion Layering: Manifester’s use of false imagery ensures plausible deniability and obscures rescue.
  • Potential Fallout: Emperor’s forces will believe Nico dead—at least temporarily.

Raw Session Notes

Session 24 – Paul GM; Todd playing Dissonance

Blurry is seeking advice on how to battle arcane opponents; went to discuss with Capone and others.

After carnival, Manifester, Dissonance, and Mantis return to their housing. Overnight, anyone living near the river—at least Manifester—hears sirens, something’s going on north of the river.

Next morning, there’s a knock on Manifester’s door, multiple people.

It’s a big goomba and Capone. Manifester lets him in, offers him a drink, makes him a whiskey. Wants to talk to him about an attack north of the river. “The Japanese attacked last night.” Japanese supervillains destroyed a makeshift camp that was holding detainees. “But there’s money to be made in these situations.” Capone explains he’s a public servant, happy to work with Uncle Sam. He’s in talks with Boss Tom about using one of his barges on the river as a makeshift jail. “Like my own little Alcatraz.”

“I got guys that can secure this thing. But the government types want to know he’s affiliated with some supers who can help if something happens. And I’m willing to pay you $50 a day EACH! And really I don’t think you’re gonna have to do much.”

Manifester agrees to let Capone say we’re helping. He gives them $250, “glad to be in business with you.”

Thomas Black (Dissonance) lives downtown. Mantis goes to check on him. When dissonance talks sometimes hear two voices. Was fighting Japanese pilot jump jet guy over Memorial, then I heard a voice more beautiful than I’ve ever heard, then I was falling. Then it’s weird half-remembered visions from then until you guys brought me home. (Every so often see double image of him.) Mantis takes him out to get hotdogs—Thomas says he doesn’t find he needs to eat anymore.

After Capone leaves, Manifester gets a call from Automatic Man who tells him he’s aware of the attack on the detention center. He believes there’s a code built into Kansas City Rose’s broadcasts. There were a lot of coded transmissions shortly before the attack on the jail. Jail holds Japanese Americans who were being sent to a detention camp. AM is surprised by the amount of chatter about it was beyond what would be expected; over the top level of operational involvement. There’s some kind of special interest there. He used Ye Olde Floppy Disc of Strategic Insight and will keep decoding it.

Manifester and Mantis (after chastising him about getting hotdogs without him) decide to meet for lunch at Town Topic for hamburgers. He updates Mantis and Dissonance on what Capone said, and he expresses his unease about detaining American citizens. Also here’s your share of the money, $40 each. Mantis says they should go free the detained Americans. Manifester suggests we should slow down, “what if I said there was another $10 in it?” … Mantis punches a guy who says something about there being “no good Japs” and they leave in a hurry.

As we leave, Mantis gets squeal on walky talky to contact his French connections. He ducks into a hotel and calls number. Person on line says “Johnny has a long mustache.” There is some conversation on other line. They tell Mantis the Japanese faction is trying to obtain a Japanese detainees who they call Nico. It means “cat.” “They’re looking for catman. Do we know what cat man do?” Unsure if he’s powers or is connected to a Japanese family. Has enough significance that emperor of Japan has personally required they acquire Nico. No idea why they want him or what his significance was. At least 3 Japanese supers were sent to try to acquire him. 1 can grow into a giant – Uziki (sumo rank champion), Lame de l'empereur (samurai) and Nehan (flying energy blaster).

We head to barge to do recon under guise of Capone sending us (which he did). On the river, there’s a gangplank out to a barge hooked to a tug. It’s afternoon. Warm spring day, sunny, water is flowing on rivers, and 40s style Cadillacs with a dozen mafiosos with machine guns setting up positions, people doing construction, and about a dozen men of Japanese descent chained to the barge. Workers are making a canopy over it, others are wheeling barrels out to the barge. Manifester realizes they’re wiring the barge up with big red barrels of gasoline to blow it up in event of attack.

Dissonance suggests we wait until cover of darkness. We find an Italian restaurant and eat until darkness. We get contacted by AM by radio. We learn several Japanese men who were liberated were found decapitated; authorities feel they were executed for not swearing allegiance to the emperor. Also chatter refers to Nico, the grandson of the emperor who is wanted for disregarding his familial obligations. May suffer a terrible fate. He is still in custody.

We head back and return to observe from distance. (Driver from Capone = Sebastian.) Now it is out in the water without gangplank. A couple simple looking bots are tending to it. No other humans on board except the Americans of Japanese descent chained under a canopy. About a half dozen red barrels on barge. Barge has a sign that says “Little Alcatraz.”

We wait until night when Dissonance has full abilities.

After talking to the guards, encouraging them to watch his jet packing. Mantis artfully flies over and lands on the tugboat. Bot operating boat greets me. Mantis says over walky talky “The crustacean is in the pot. The crustacean is in the pot. Over.”

Manifester has moved down to the rail bridge. Lined up with the barge. Manifester creates illusion of a large boat coming up behind them, and Dissonance amplifies a huge horn noise. Mantis yanks on anchor off the boat and tells bot to drive forward. Bot runs the boat forward and we’re picking up speed.

There is a sound along the rail bridge of the metal creaking a little. See someone flying through the air toward the barge, a large man. Those on barge make a CON save. Oziki jumped from the rail bridge and slams onto the barge, kicks up, pitches Dissonance into the water. Whole barge wobbles.

Manifester creates the portal right at edge of barge.

Dissonance flies out of the water, high enough he doesn’t think anyone can reach him, and tries to assess what’s going on.

From west, a bright light appears with a woman in white & red robe in center, flying towards the barge.

Mantis heads onto barge and greets Ozeki. He says prepare to die. Mantis grabs a barrel and starts juggling it, trying to get Ozeki to move forward.

Mafiosos start firing guns at boat. Ozeki gets hit a few times but doesn’t bother him. Mantis dodges and yells at them to knock it off.

Manifester attempts to create illusion for mafiosos of people up the bank from them shouting in Japanese and shooting at them. Fails.

Ozeki grabs chain holding the prisoners, rips it from the boat, throws chain into the water. 3 fell in and chain starts pulling the others into the water. Mutters something about “suitable death for a traitor.”

Dissonance blasts Ozeki in the face – there’s a distortion in the air and a low thump noise that everyone else feels in their chest. Ozeki’s whole body jiggles when he’s hit with it. He staggers backwards. Manages to keep his feet and stay on the barge (10 dmg).

A beam of radiant energy from Nehan strikes the mafiosos; two Cadillacs explode, several collapse to the ground and the rest flee.

Manifester creates illusion that she’s blind on Nehan; she’s visually impaired but not completely blind.

Ozeki charges at Mantis with sumo style. Mantis doesn’t lose or gain ground, no damage taken.

There’s a shimmering above the barge. A samurai steps through it, pulls a small sword and drives it through the chain, stopping their descend. He says something like “Time to die, Nico.”

Barge continues moving toward the portal.

Dissonance flies down over the people in the water, grabs the chain, and lifts the 3 guys so their heads are above water. He moves towards the barge so that they can scramble on.

Mantis pushing against Ozeki says “I told you I had a power like yours” then shrinks while pitching him over top into the water. Ozeki curses. Mantis 2-foot tall catches the barrel of gasoline he was juggling one-handed.

Manifester attempts to create illusion for samurai that there are ninjas crawling out of the water to attack him. Fails.

Very ceremoniously samurai draws katana while stepping towards one of the prisoners, ready to decapitate who we assume is Nico.

Mantis launches the gasoline barrel at Emperor’s Blade… whiffs. Manifester fails to make him think he’s being stung by bees. Dissonance flies through Nico, who is on his knees, and unphases in front of EB. “He is under the protection of the United States.” “The United States will be destroyed by the Emperor.” Blade swings on Dissonance – 8 dmg.

Nehan begins firing wildly while visually impaired. Misses everyone. Steam rises from the river, freaking Mantis out.

The tugboat begins being pulled into the river as Ozeki tries to climb up it.

Manifester retries the swarm of bees illusion, succeeds this time. Blade takes 6 dmg. He shimmers and disappears, saying to Nehan “kill them all” as he disappears. Light radiating from her starts to expand. Manifester yells “quick get in the portal.”

Mantis grabs the chain, leaps through the portal dragging the prisoners with him using ridiculous strength. Success!!!

Dissonance blasts Nehan for 5 dmg. then flies through the portal. Manifester closes the portal.

Blinding flash, beam of energy, hits the barge which explodes, gasoline explodes, a huge wave crests in the river, Ozeki flies, tugboat capsizes.

Manifester creates illusion that the prisoners are still on the barge. Everyone will be convinced that the prisoners were incinerated in the destructive blast, including the Japanese supers who believe Nico’s dead.

Dissonance is silencing the noise of the prisoners being pulled up on the chain.
