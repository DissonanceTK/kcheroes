Session 8 - 64105

Session Summary

In-Game Date: April 11, 2022
Dimension: 1

Key Events

  • Exit from the Dome:
    The team departs the geodesic dome after Dr. Goodfellow shouts “Eureka!” from within. In their vehicle, they find a locked metal suitcase keyed to Mantis Shrimp’s thumbprint, containing:

    • $250 cash
    • ID for “Claude”
    • Miniature firearm
    • Hazmat suit
    • Backpack device
    • Karate gi with black belt

  • Strange Shifts in Reality:
    When Manifester starts the car, the usual “Hysteria” track is replaced by “All I Need Is a Miracle.” The car also appears cleaner, with upgraded fast-food wrappers.
    They spot unusual changes across Kansas City:

    • Roadside celebrity cutouts featuring Mantis Shrimp
    • A new light rail system
    • Barry’s trailer looks different
    • Radio references President Harris, not Biden

  • The Trout Blimp:
    A fish-shaped blimp marked Freddy the Fisherman’s Trout House advertises a phone number: 816-317-576*.
    Calling reveals the blimp was stolen. Mantis vows to retrieve it.

  • Chaos on the Bridge:
    The team heads toward the Buck O’Neil Bridge, where the giant trout blimp is wedged into the bridge entrance, halting traffic. Someone has written “I am a bomb” on its side.
    Templar senses movement atop the bridge. REO Speedwagon’s “Time for Me to Fly” begins playing, compelling people to dance uncontrollably.

  • Arrival of Meat Shield:
    A shapeless, gooey figure descends from above, wearing a tuxedo t-shirt.
    The creature—later identified as Meat Shield—contains moving human forms within it. One victim briefly pushes through the skin and screams before vanishing again.
    Meat Shield greets Templar:

    “So nice of you to join the dance party.”

  • Battle on the Bridge:

    • Mantis Shrimp activates the backpack device, discovering it grants flight.
    • A speedster abducts a bystander as a car phases through the bridge.
    • Manifester attempts to blink the dancers to safety but fails.
    • Templar shoots a nearby boom box to stop the music—unsuccessfully.
    • Manifester creates an illusion of silence, breaking the crowd’s trance.
    • Among the revelers, a new villain—Mosh Pit—is revealed.

  • Combat Escalates:

    • Meat Shield taunts: “You found my colleague: Mosh Pit!”
    • Mosh Pit fires at Manifester but misses.
    • Manifester retaliates with a psychic energy wave, knocking Mosh Pit unconscious.
    • Templar shoots Mosh Pit’s weapon away—his bullet ricochets, striking Fire Cube (Molotov) as he approaches.
    • Meat Shield slingshots himself off the bridge and flees.
    • Mantis Shrimp drains hydrogen from the blimp to prevent explosion while Templar blasts incoming fireballs.
    • Fire Cube retreats.
    • Automatic Man intercepts police chatter confirming the gooey attacker’s name: Meat Shield.

Heroes

  • Mantis Shrimp – Gains flight using the mysterious backpack; prevents a blimp explosion.
  • Templar – Uses precision shooting to neutralize threats and save civilians.
  • Manifester – Disrupts the mind-controlled crowd through illusion and bravado.
  • Automatic Man – Monitors communications, confirming villain identities and coordinating the team.

Villains

  • Meat Shield – A horrifying, goo-based entity containing live victims.
  • Mosh Pit – Ally of Meat Shield; uses sound and crowd frenzy as a weapon.
  • Fire Cube – Returns briefly before being driven off.

Notes

This session marks the heroes’ first shift into Dimension 1, where subtle yet disturbing differences define their world—alternate leadership, infrastructure, and cultural echoes.
The fight on the Buck O’Neil Bridge introduces new enemies tied to sound and flesh-based mutation, while surreal humor (Manifester’s reactions, Mantis’s impulsive heroism) tempers the dark tone.
Dr. Goodfellow’s “Eureka!” and the bizarre suitcase hint at continuity between dimensions.

Raw Session Notes

(Note: this session and the next several are in Dimension 1.)

Session 8: 11/12/23

We exit the geodesic dome; overhear Goodfellow saying “eureka!” as we leave.
Insider the car: a locked metal suitcase with Mantis sized thumb print:
• Cash $250, ID for Claude, tiny gun, hazmat suit, backpack-type device, karate gi w black belt
When Manifester starts car, it plays “All I Need is a Miracle” instead of “Hysteria,” freaking out Manifester. Has better fast-food wrappers in car than should be there. Traveling see Mantis Shrimp among celebrity cutouts on roadside. Barry’s trailer is also different. There’s a new light rail system in KC. Automatic Man picks up on radio references to President Harris instead of Biden.
In distance see medium-sized fish-designed blimp, trout-like – advertising Freddy the Fisherman’s Trout House. Phone 816-317-576*.
Call the number and learn the blimp has been stolen! Mantis agrees to retrieve it.
Manifester follows towards the Buck O’Neil bridge. It shoves its way into the bridge entrance, stopping traffic. Giant happy trout. Someone blacked out some of its teeth and wrote “I am a bomb” on it. It is on far side of bridge from us.
Templar senses someone is on top of the bridge.
In distance hear REO Speedwagon “Time for Me to Fly.” People hearing the music get out of their cars and dance. Music has menacing feel.
Something begins lowering itself down from the top of the bridge like silly putty wearing clothes: tuxedo t-shirt. Is larger than normal person and has moving blobs within it. Has crazy eyes and greets Templar “so nice of you to join the dance party.” A blob pushes through skin and it’s a person who screams.
Mantis puts on backpack. It gives him wings/power of flight.
A couple turns into combat, someone runs past at hyperspeed, jumps up, car goes fuzzy focus; they grab someone out of the car and the car phases through the bridge.
Manifester tries to use blink to create portals to transport dancers away from harm—fails. They fizzle out.
Templar shoots boom box… the music did not stop. Manifester notes one of the revelers chuckles at the attempt to silence the music.
Manifester creates illusion of no music, breaking the trance of the dancers. Manifester points out the baddie among the revelers. Gooey guy says “You found my colleague: Mosh Pit!” then swats at Automatic Man.
In distance, see someone flying towards us, looks like Fire Cube. Mosh Pit pulls gun on Manifester but misses. Manifester blasts Mosh Pit with an energy wave, knocking him out.
Templar shoots gun out of Mosh Pit’s hand, bullet ricochets to hit Fire Cube/Molotov.
Gooey slingshots himself away.
Mantis sucks the hydrogen out of the blimp as fireballs go at it, Templar blasting them out of the sky.
Molotov runs away.
Automatic Man hears police chatter: “Meat Shield” is gooey guy.
