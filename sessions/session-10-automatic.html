Session 10 - Automatic

Session Summary

In-Game Date: April 11, 2022
Dimension: 1

Key Events

  • Jerry’s Observation:
    Jerry becomes increasingly aware of the heroes’ off-world origins, confirming they are from an alternate universe.
    His cold, logical demeanor contrasts with Blurry’s enthusiasm and risk-taking leadership.

  • Manifester’s Celica on the News:
    Manifester begins receiving frantic texts asking why his Celica is on the news—broadcast live in a low-speed police chase through Kansas City.
    The driver is none other than Automatic Man, who appears disoriented and confused.

    [GM note: Automatic Man’s “training simulations” were based on Grand Theft Auto.]

  • A Psychic Connection:
    Despite the distance, Manifester feels a telepathic link to the car’s CD player, suggesting his powers can extend through objects he’s emotionally bonded with.

  • The Chase and the Pit Stop:
    The car is headed from River Market toward West Bottoms, driving erratically.
    Before pursuing, the group stops at Gates BBQ for food—an appropriately KC decision—then meanders after Automatic Man.
    They notice an A.R.I.E.S. vehicle tailing them but breaking off when they reach the West Bottoms.

  • The Scene at 1104 Union Ave:
    The area is desolate.

    • A Blackhawk helicopter lies on its side, smashed as though by blunt trauma.
    • A massive magnetic crane looms nearby, holding Manifester’s Celica aloft by its undercarriage.
    • Automatic Man is inside, seemingly paralyzed or exhausted, possibly due to the magnetic field.
    • Smoke pours from the crane.
    • Below it stands a heavyset figure in improvised armor, controlling the swirling debris around them with telekinetic or magnetic power.
    • Templar detects multiple threats in surrounding buildings:
      • 5th floor (right building): weapon being readied.
      • 3rd floor (left building): chains clanking, possible captive.
      • Ahead: a cyclone forming, pulling in rebar and debris.

  • Combat Erupts:
    Initiative Order: Templar, Manifester, Villains, Mantis Shrimp.

    • Templar sets the Humvee to defense mode, bails out, and takes cover behind a burned-out car.
    • Manifester teleports to the 4th floor of a nearby building.
    • Automatic gunfire erupts toward Templar—bullets hit the wreckage but miss him.
    • The cyclone intensifies; the crane groans under strain.
    • Mantis Shrimp steps into the swirling debris, catches a flying radiator, and begins bending it into a weapon while trying to talk the armored foe down. The enemy responds by flipping him off.

  • Templar’s Precision Shot:
    Hearing a gunman cock a weapon, Templar fires through the sound—his bullet hits the shooter in the mouth, dropping him through the window.
    Both the gun and body are pulled into the debris cyclone.

  • Manifester’s Interference:
    From above, Manifester spots the armored foe clearly and realizes he’s controlling the debris field.
    He attempts to impose an illusion of blindness, but fails.
    The crane’s motor begins to overheat and will fail in three rounds.

  • The Cyclone Grows:
    The stocky armored villain tightens control over the debris, creating a funnel cloud.
    Mantis is swept up and suspended in midair, the radiator pole fusing magnetically to the crane’s dish.

    (Ion pack rule reminder: grants flight for 1d4 rounds, then 1d4 rounds of recovery.)

  • Distress Call:
    From the building on the left, Templar hears a woman crying, “Help me!” followed by a man’s warning:

    “Woman, don’t get them involved—they’ll just get killed.”
    Templar starts climbing to intervene.

  • The Cyclone Strikes Back:
    The armored foe launches the debris storm toward Templar. A corpse smashes into him, dealing minor damage (4 points), and he’s sucked into the cyclone, spinning helplessly.
    Mantis clings desperately to the embedded radiator pole beneath the suspended Celica.

  • End Scene:
    Mantis climbs higher, grabs the car frame, and tries to wrench it free from the magnet. The vehicle only shifts position—now driver side up.
    Inside, Automatic Man tumbles, his stunned face slamming against the passenger window—right next to Mantis.
    Mantis ends the session with 6 stun points, the storm raging and the crane beginning to fail.

Heroes

  • Templar – Tactical precision; identifies threats, eliminates a gunman, and braves the cyclone.
  • Larry Harry Jones – Teleports into position, manipulates the field with illusions, but fails key psychic rolls.
  • Mantis Shrimp – Physically engages the debris storm, attempts negotiation and heroics while midair.
  • Automatic Man – Found incapacitated within Manifester’s Celica, likely disabled by the magnetic field.
  • Blurry – Mentioned but off-screen this session; his team remains in contact.

Supporting Characters

  • Jerry – Coldly rational AI, recognizes the team’s extradimensional status.
  • Armored Villain (unnamed) – Controls debris via magnetic or telekinetic force; central antagonist of the scene.
  • Unknown Captives – Possibly civilians or prisoners; one female voice heard pleading for help.

Notes

This session blends cinematic chaos with dark humor and heavy foreshadowing.
The heroes face a magnetic storm of chaotic forces, literal and figurative: dimensional dislocation, machine influence, and psychic overstimulation.
Manifester’s growing psychic connection to technology, especially his Celica, hints at deeper cross-dimensional resonance.
The magnetic crane, the storm, and the armored foe set up a high-stakes cliffhanger—leaving the team battered, disoriented, and still mid-battle.
