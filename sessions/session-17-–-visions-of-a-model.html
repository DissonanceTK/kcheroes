Session 17 – Visions of a Model

Session Summary

In-Game Date:
Dimension: 1

Key Events

  • Lantern Man on the Road:
    On the return drive from the drone mission, Manifester spots a lanky man carrying a lantern whose glow matches the light from his recurring forest dreams. When he points him out to Templar, the man vanishes.

  • Back at the Workshop:
    At Madman Markham #2’s hideout, MM2 and Automatic Man collaborate on adapting the drone software so
    Manifester's illusions can affect AI. MM2 handles hardware modifications while AM manages software integration.

  • A Call from Marvin:
    Templar receives word from his informant Marvin, a street sweeper, that two FBI agents investigating him disappeared after entering The Pecks Building downtown. The Bureau appears to be ignoring the case.

  • Cover Story and Entry:
    To investigate, the team recruits realtor Max Jones of Mojo Realty to show them the property, posing as representatives for Mantis Shrimp, who claims he wants to open a “bookshop / cat sanctuary / mystery-solving business.”

  • Inside the Pecks Building:
    The ground floor, a former bank, now contains 3D dioramas of battles from across dimensions — some from the heroes’ past, others yet to occur.
    A grey-haired man named The Architect greets them, claiming to “see” these scenes as visions of heroism. He built the models and says a new battle will soon unfold in this very building.
    Max flees in terror, but The Architect remains calm even when Manifester reveals his identity.

  • The Architect’s Visions:

    • He may see future events or alternate timelines.
    • A coming battle here will end in victory, “but at a cost.”
    • The foes include a strange man with tentacles and his victims — one resembling the missing FBI agent.
    • The upper floors (2–4) have been reshaped into a theater laced with living cables that attack intruders; many who ascend never return.
    • He references Mosh Pit and his associate Professor Maelstrom, suggesting the killer may be tied to them.
    • He shows a sketch of the creature — a muscular humanoid with four tentacles sprouting from his back — and confirms the FBI agents went upstairs seeking him.

  • Into the Models:
    Each hero places their miniature on The Architect’s display and instantly vanishes to that corresponding position in the real building:

    • Mantis Shrimp (as Claude Crevette) on the fourth-floor balcony.
    • Manifester on the opposite balcony.
    • Templar in the “Critic’s Box,” offering superior sightlines.
      As they fade, The Architect calmly sits and begins sketching the unfolding scene.

  • The Theater Awakens:
    The heroes materialize in a dim, stage-lit auditorium. Mantis Shrimp hears the creak of a swinging pendulum; Templar spots a woman bound beneath a razor-sharp blade and shoots the cable, freeing her before it can strike.

  • The Tentacled Killer Attacks:
    A monstrous tentacled man storms the balcony to assault Claude. Manifester delivers a psychic blast that staggers him, as a mutated FBI agent with mechanical limbs climbs the opposite wall toward Manifester.

  • Flash and Fall:
    Mantis Shrimp fires his tiny pistol — a blinding flash hurls the tentacled killer backward off the balcony. He crashes onto the stage, his severed limbs entangling the FBI creature below. Both die as the living cables shrivel into dust.

  • Rescue and Aftermath:
    Templar cuts the surviving female FBI agent free, silences her attempts to speak with a cold “You owe me,” and walks away.
    Mantis Shrimp phones Max Jones quipping, “This place has potential. Let’s put in an offer.”
    Manifester questions the rescued agent, who confirms:

    • The tentacled man was a serial killer they’d been tracking.
    • An anonymous tip (likely from Mosh Pit’s network) led them here.
    • The killer’s abilities echoed Mosh Pit’s control over life and death.

Heroes

  • Mantis Shrimp – Investigates as Claude Crevette, defeats tentacle killer with precision shot.
  • Manifester – Reveals his identity to the Architect, delivers psychic damage in battle.
  • Templar – Protects the captive and ensures their survival; remains pragmatic and coldly efficient.
  • Automatic Man – Works with MM2 on drone-AI conversion before the investigation begins.

Villains and Threats

  • Tentacle Killer – Serial murderer and anomaly, possibly tied to Mosh Pit’s cult-like following.
  • Mutated FBI Agent (Nosferatu) – Victim of the killer, reanimated with mechanical augmentations.
  • Cables/Theater Construct – Controlled extensions of the tentacled man; dissipate after his death.
  • Professor Maelstrom – Mentioned as a future threat by the Architect.

Other Characters

  • Madman Markham #2 – Begins new tech integration with data from drone mission.
  • Marvin – Street sweeper informant for Templar; tips about missing FBI agents.
  • Max Jones – Realtor for Mojo Realty, flees the scene in terror.
  • The Architect – An anomaly who foresees and models dimensional battles; possibly undead or immortal.
  • Female FBI Agent – Rescued survivor, confirms Mosh Pit’s involvement.
  • Mosh Pit – Referenced; rumored to have returned after repeated deaths.

Notes

  • The Architect may be an unintentional conduit of dimensional echoes, replaying events across universes.
  • His connection to Mosh Pit and Professor Maelstrom hints at an upcoming multi-dimensional convergence.
  • The Pecks Building seems alive—its layout changes and mimics the entities within.
  • Mantis Shrimp’s humor and real estate quip underline the team’s growing comfort with the surreal horror of Dimension 1.
