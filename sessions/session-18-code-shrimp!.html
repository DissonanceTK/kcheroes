Session 18 - CODE SHRIMP!

Session Summary

Key Events

  • Calm Before the Chaos:
    Mantis Shrimp teaches martial arts to ten-year-olds at his dojo. Templar investigates his standing with the Church (persona non grata after the padre’s death) and the missing FBI agents. Manifester wrestles with staying in this dimension’s luxury with Rooftop versus returning to his Riverside trailer and Celica.

  • Distress Call:
    Rooftop calls Manifester from the Wallstreet Building (1101 Walnut), claiming she’s pinned and a battle is raging outside. Manifester alerts the team; Mantis relays the code: “1101 Walnut. Go now. CODE SHRIMP!”

  • Scene on Arrival:
    The area is a disaster: overturned self-driving bus, ruptured lines, structural damage to Wallstreet’s support pillar. Peachy (super-strong) and 40K (density manipulator) are brawling; 24-7 and Streetcar are unconscious nearby. Battle Chasers YouTubers film as police, fire, and Khaki Tacs secure the zone.

  • Escalation:
    Manifester pranks a mocking YouTuber with an ant-swarm illusion; attempts de-escalation with Peachy/40K. Templar orders “STAND DOWN!” over the PA. Peachy rips the supercar’s turret and hurls it; the street collapses, swallowing Peachy, 40K, and Templar’s car. Mantis Shrimp presses for restraint—gets thrown skyward by 40K.

  • Inside the Building — The Lantern Man:
    Manifester portals into Wallstreet to reach Leandra; the light shifts to the golden glow from his forest dreams. Leandra vanishes, replaced by Marcus (“the man with the lantern”), who says, “There are no illusions. To be perceived is to be real,” and proposes “working together.” Manifester resists and later blinks back outside.

  • Archangel Appears:
    A crackling, unconscious Archangel floats ~40 ft up, flinging lightning like a storm front. 40K pleads with her; Peachy panics; Templar lines up shots. Mantis urges 40K to help divert or ground her and to support the building.

  • Containment Attempts:
    Mantis/Peachy try bracing with the bus; Templar holds fire; Manifester projects a calm meadow illusion that lowers Archangel’s output. Marcus re-energizes her with a yellow beam; Manifester yells, “Shoot this guy!” and Templar wounds Marcus, breaking his focus.

  • Seconds from Ruin:
    40K is blasted trying to reach her. Mantis’s ion pack is zapped mid-flight. Templar wounds Archangel without stopping her drift toward the building. Manifester lands a perfect meadow illusion (nat 20): tendrils vanish, Archangel faints. Mantis misses the catch; 40K teleports mid-fall to grab her and craters on landing, then teleports away with her.

  • Aftermath:
    Peachy menaces Khaki Tacs with the bus, then backs down. Manifester floors Marcus with a judo throw after a flubbed exchange (“Stay away from me, you creepy weirdo”). Peachy and Mantis help free Templar’s car; the team departs.

Heroes

  • Mantis Shrimp — De-escalation attempts; structural triage with Peachy; multiple jetpack risks; coordination with 40K.
  • Manifester — Civilian extractions via portals; ant-swarm prank; key calming illusion on Archangel; confronts Marcus.
  • Templar — Scene control and precision fire; disables Marcus to stop his interference; manages perimeter amid infrastructure collapse.

Villains and Threats

  • 40K — Density manipulator (40,000 lbs); volatile when provoked; devoted to Archangel.
  • Peachy — Super-strong brawler; not a villain, but destructive in the moment.
  • Archangel — Unconscious conduit of massive electrical energy; a natural-disaster-level threat when active.
  • Marcus — Lantern-bearing psychic/influencer tied to Manifester’s dreams; attempts to manipulate events (and Manifester).
  • Khaki Tacs — Armed presence; potential escalation point.

Other Characters

  • Rooftop — The “distress call,” later revealed as an illusion at Marcus’s locus.
  • 24-7 — Found unconscious near the fight.
  • Streetcar — Unconscious in the nearby park.
  • Battle Chasers (YouTubers) — On-site streamers; one suffers ant-swarm illusion.
  • Police and Fire crews — Manage perimeter and evacuations.

Notes

  • Marcus appears to project/anchor illusions independent of Manifester and to amplify or dampen powered states (yellow beam into Archangel).
  • Archangel behaves like an unconscious elemental; illusions altering emotional/ambient context can modulate her output.
  • Significant collateral damage around 1101 Walnut; support pillar compromised but total collapse averted.
  • Public perception shaped by Battle Chasers footage (including Manifester’s ant gag and the supercar damage).
