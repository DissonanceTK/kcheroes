Raw Session Notes

Session 27, “Little Alcatraz”

9/7/2025

This is a draft.  Some of the details may be wrong or out of order, such as what information the heroes got from the French Intelligence Officer versus what the heroes learned from Automatic Man.  Also, the sequence of events in the combat is probably wrong and incomplete.  We can update and correct it with notes from the players.

A plan starts out badly and ends beautifully.

In the 1949 Campaign:

The morning after the night at The Carnival, there is a knock at Manifester’s door.  It’s Capone with several of his tough guys.  Capone explains that in the early morning hours a makeshift jail north of the river was attacked by “Jap Supervillains.”  The jail housed “Japs” that were about to get transported to a “camp.”  The jail was partially destroyed and many of the “Japs” escaped.  Capone explains that there’s money to be made in helping Uncle Sam.  Capone says he has equipment and facilities through his connections and is having a river barge converted to a floating jail to hold the “Japs.”  He calls it “Little Alcatraz.”  Capone explains that Government pencil-pushers always have demands.  Capone asks Manifester to lend his “crew” to guarding the jail, so Capone can get approval with the Government, since “Jap super villains” attacked the jail, the pencil-pushers want superheroes signed on with the private jail.  Capone says he’ll pay each of the heroes $50 a day.  Manifester does not like the idea but agrees.  Capone gives him $250.

Meanwhile, Captain France goes to the home of Thomas Black (Dissonance) to check on him.  Thomas describes his memory of being attacked by a Japanese man with a “jump pack.”  Thomas remembers falling, but his memory is vague after that until the Heroes rescued him from The Carnival.  Captain France suggests they get food.

Back at Manifester’s, after Capone leaves, Automatic Man calls Manifester.  Automatic Man has detected a code in Kansas City Rose’s broadcasts.  He has also been monitoring Government communications.  He has not entirely cracked the Rose Code, but he has determined that there was a lot of chatter associated with the attack on the makeshift jail.  Automatic Man used Ye Olde Floppy Disk and determined that the amount of chatter is beyond what would be expected based on the nature of the attack mission.  There must be something more going on.

Manifester contacts Captain France by walkie-talkie.  Manifester, Captain France, and Dissonance meet at Town Topic Hamburgers.  Manifester tells them about the job from Capone and the call from Automatic Man.  Manifester gives each of them $40.  They get food.  Captain France says the heroes must save the Japanese-Americans.  The three heroes talk about what to do.  A man sitting at the diner bar says something about locking all the Japs up.  Captain France disagrees with him.  The man yells, “Remember the USS Arizona!”  Captain France punches him, knocking him out.  The diners flee.  Soon there are police sirens in the distance.  The three heroes leave the restaurant after leaving a big tip.

Captain France gets a special signal on his walkie-talkie that tells him to call his French Intelligence Officer.  He calls a special number from a hotel pay phone.  After some confusion over the correct response to “Johnny has a long mustache,” Captain France learns that Japanese Supervillains attacked the jail.  That call combined with another discussion with Automatic Man gets the heroes information about three Japanese Anomalies: Ozeki, Nihon, and Emperor’s Blade.  Automatic Man tells them that several of the Japanese-Americans who were “liberated” from the makeshift jail were later found decapitated.  Automatic Man also tells them that the Japanese Anomalies were looking for the Emperor’s Grandson among the Japanese prisoners.  The Japanese call him “Nico,” which means “cat.”  The Japanese Anomalies intend to kill him as a traitor. 

The Heroes get a ride from Sebastian, the driver provided to them by Capone, to the river to view Little Alcatraz.  The Barge is in the Missouri River in an industrial area a few hundred west (upstream) from a railroad bridge and a street bridge.  There are about a dozen of Capone’s henchmen on the bank with machine guns.  There is walkway out to the barge.  Workers are building a canopy on the barge.  Other workers are placing red barrels on the barge.  Manifester recalls something Capone said and realizes the red barrels are gasoline.  There are a dozen men of Japanese descent chained together and chained to the barge.  Two bots are working on the barge.  A tugboat is keeping the barge in place. 

The Heroes discuss a plan and go get Italian food.  The heroes plan more and come back after dark.  Manifester teleports onto the rail bridge east of Little Alcatraz.  The barge is now anchored in the middle of the river with the tugboat hooked to the barge.  There are two groups of Capone Henchmen with machine guns on the bank on each side of the river.  The Heroes plan to have Manifester create a portal and then move the barge toward the portal and teleport the prisoners through the portal. 

Captain France tells the Capone Henchman that he is going on the barge to guard it.  He uses his jetpack to fly Rocketeer-style onto the barge.

Dissonance flies onto the barge.  Captain France goes into the cabin of the tugboat. 

Manifester creates an illusion of large ship headed toward the tugboat and barge from the west.  Dissonance creates a loud noise to go with the illusion. 

The robot in the tugboat begins moving the tugboat and barge downstream toward the rail bridge. 

Manifester hears the metal of the rail bridge whine as someone who is very heavy leaps from the rail bridge onto the barge.  It is Ozeki, the Japanese sumo.  The barge pitches when Ozeki lands on it.  Dissonance is thrown into the river.

Manifester creates the portal downstream from the barge.

Captain France moves from the tugboat to the barge and politely greets Ozeki.  Ozeki tells Captain France to prepare to die.

Dissonance flies up from the water and blasts Ozeki with sonic energy, causing his giant body to undulate and quiver.

Ozeki rips the chain out of the barge and pitches it into the river with enough force that three of the prisoners and pulled into the river and others are starting to slide toward the edge of the barge. 

A woman begins flying in from the west toward the barge.  She is wearing white and red robes and is surrounded by bright light.  It is Nihon. 

Capone’s Henchmen on the north bank of the river begin firing their machine guns at the barge.  They hit only Ozeki.  His huge body ripples again, absorbing much of the damage with only minimal harm to him. 

Manifester tries to create an illusion of other people for Capone’s Henchmen to fire at and fails. 

Ozeki charges at Captain France sumo-style.  Captain France successfully holds his ground near the edge of the barge.

Dissonance flies over and lifts the chain that is in the river, getting the men in the river above water and close enough to the barge to grab it. 

The north side of the river explodes with an energy blast from Nihon.  Two Cadillacs explode into the air and collapse again burning on the bank.  Several of the henchmen are killed.  The others on the north bank flee.

Manifester creates an illusion impairing Nihon’s vision.

Captain France has a sumo-style contest of strength against Ozeki and succeeds in throwing Ozeki into the river.   

Nihon blasts a beam of light into the river, creating a massive steam explosion, which almost freaks out Captain France, but he composes himself.

On the barge, a space in the air shimmers and opens.  A man in a samurai outfit steps onto the barge.  It is Emperor’s Blade.  He drives a knife into a link of chain pinning it in place and draws his sword and stands over one of the prisoners.  He says something angry in Japanese and raises his sword prepared to decapitate him.

Captain France tries to throw a barrel of gas (that he’s been holding since he politely greeted Ozeki) at Emperor’s Blade.  He misses.  Manifester tries to create an illusion of bees stinging Emperor’s Blade.  He fails.  Dissonance flies over and phases through the prisoner, placing himself in the way of Emperor’s Blade.  Emperor’s Blade slashes Dissonance.  Dissonance is seriously wounded.   

The tugboat begins to sink as Ozeki grabs it to try to get out of the river.

Manifester tries again to create an illusion of bees stinging Emperor’s Blade and succeeds, doing significant damage to Emperor’s Blade. 

Emperor’s Blade yells something in Japanese.  The air near Emperor’s Blade shimmers again and he disappears.  Nihon begins to glow brighter.  The heroes realize this is bad.

Dissonance blast Nihon with sonic energy, but Nihon still glows brighter.

Captain France runs, grabs the chain that is attached to the prisoner, and leaps through the portal, pulling the prisoners with him and transporting himself and them onto the railway bridge.  Dissonance flies through the portal, transporting himself to the railway bridge.  At the last instance, Manifester creates an illusion of the prisoners still stuck on the barge as the barge explodes from Nihon’s energy blast attack.  The massive explosion lights up the night sky from the combined power of the energy attack and the exploding barrels of gasoline.

On the railway bridge, Captain France pulls up the chain to lift prisoners who are hanging from it.  Dissonance silences the sound of the chain and the prisoners until all the prisoners are safely on the railway bridge.
