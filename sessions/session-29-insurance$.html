Session 29 - Insurance$

Session Summary

In-Game Date: Fall, 1949
Dimension: 45

Key Events

  • Aftermath on the Bridge:
    Following the destruction of “Little Alcatraz,” the heroes regroup on the rail bridge with the rescued detainees, including Nico, grandson of the Emperor of Japan. Returning home would be dangerous; they’re all presumed dead.

    Mantis Shrimp suggests asking Al Capone for safe housing, but Manifester doubts the mobster would hide fugitives. Mantis volunteers his own place instead.

  • Safehouse and Cover Story:
    The group escorts Nico and the other detainees to Mantis’ home under the guise of newly arrived travelers from France, hiring cabs from the airport to sell the illusion. Nico expresses deep gratitude for their rescue.

    Once settled, Mantis contacts French HQ via coded line. They rebuke him for “assaulting an American man in a hamburger joint,” warning that his behavior could trigger a diplomatic incident. They order him to meet with Dr. Louie Breshears for psychological evaluation.

    They also relay that a Jessica Harman called the French Embassy urgently seeking Mantis about “the Japanese men.” She seemed sane enough that the message was forwarded through official channels. Mantis explodes at the suggestion he “compose himself,” slamming the receiver and storming out.

  • The Majestic Meeting:
    The team reconvenes at The Majestic with Automatic Man, who has gathered intelligence.

    • Three two-headed calves have been reported near Parkville — a symptom of reality instability.
    • He has deciphered part of the Kansas City Rose transmissions: they’re meant to demoralize the public, erode confidence in safety, and recruit American defectors.
    • The Japanese superhumans have been attacking from the air using jetpacks, though ion resonance doesn’t exist yet in this universe. The technology’s fine motor precision suggests someone of exceptional skill—perhaps not of this dimension.
    • Their next target appears to be McClellon’s Art Supply, scheduled for attack during Saturday’s children’s art day.

  • Capone and the Payoff:
    Capone bursts in, grinning. “You guys are great! I’m making so much money! I had that barge insured!” He peels off $200 apiece (plus Manifester’s remaining $40). As he departs, he nudges Mantis: “She looks like she wants to talk to you.”
    Enter Jessica Harman — dark hair, tired eyes, composed but weary.

  • Jessica’s Revelation:
    Jessica confirms she’s Nico’s daughter, a reporter for the Kansas City Times. Passing as Native American, she’s been covering the internment scandal. She photographed the barge explosion, believing all aboard were dead, and sent the film to be developed. The editor called — the photos clearly show Japanese men hanging from the railway bridge.
    The Times plans to publish the story tomorrow afternoon, which could expose the entire operation.

    Mantis asks Capone, hypothetically, what he’d do if the detainees were still alive. Capone replies he “wouldn’t like the attention” and confirms there’s no life insurance on them. He hints that he could “talk to someone at the Times” if necessary.

    Mantis tells Jessica that, if she wishes, he can reunite her with her father. She is shaken but intrigued.

  • Lingering Threats:
    The heroes consider how to handle the coming media storm, while Mantis and Automatic Man reflect on the advanced jetpack tech — a signature they’ve seen before.
    Both recall a name whispered in connection to impossible machines and dimensional craftwork: The Architect.
