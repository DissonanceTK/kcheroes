Session 16 – Mad Men

Session Summary

In-Game Date:
Dimension: 1

Key Events

  • Into the Workshop:
    Madman lowers his weapon and invites Manifester, Templar, and Mantis Shrimp into his rustic North Kansas City workshop while Automatic Man remains “asleep” in a nearby car.

  • Oscar’s Hospitality:
    Inside, they meet Oscar, Madman’s gruff but friendly associate, grilling bologna sandwiches and serving lemonade. Nearby, the anomalies ActiVADER and Boba Fett are restrained—Fett sedated, ActiVADER chained to the floor.

  • Madman #2’s Revelation:
    The man identifies himself as Madman Markham #2 (MM2) from Dimension 2 and explains the multiversal situation.

    • Madman Markham #1 (native to this dimension) works with Eric Greitens.
    • Madman #2 invented an interdimensional “elevator,” while his counterpart can pull beings between realities.
    • In his world, Greitens #2 became Emperor of North America after a coup; MM1 brought MM2 here to keep that tech out of Greitens #2’s hands.
    • Madman #2 can “read” information across dimensions and found the heroes after their victory over Greitens in Dimension 24.

  • A Dangerous Plan:
    Madman #2 warns that Eric Greitens and Madman #1 intend to bring 4–5 dozen Automatic Men into this world, enslave them, and use them to conquer the planet.

  • Operation Stingray — The Mission Brief:
    MM2 requests help assaulting MM1’s compound but first needs data from ionic drones recharging near the Heart of America Bridge.
    The drones deactivate self-destructs during charging; the team must locate a unit’s serial number and use MM2’s passcode to access its port.

  • Approach and Positions:
    Mantis Shrimp rides in on a trout blimp but is grabbed by a crow and dropped into the river.
    Automatic Man leaps from the Heart of America Bridge, landing atop the charging platform and triggering alarms.
    Templar sets up sniper overwatch on the Kit Bond Bridge.
    Manifester poses as a fisherman on the pedestrian span, preparing illusions.

  • Battle at the Bridge:
    Templar ricochet-snipes multiple security bots.
    Mantis Shrimp is ambushed by Platypus, a cryptid from D24, but Manifester stuns it with a psychic blast.
    Automatic Man identifies the drone’s serial number and opens its hatch after deciphering Oscar’s bad handwriting.
    24-7 arrives mid-fight; MM2 groans, recognizing him as a major threat.

  • Illusions and Errors:
    Manifester’s illusions misfire spectacularly—first painting glowing arrows over allies’ heads, then marking himself for Khaki Tac fire.
    Mantis Shrimp and Automatic Man endure heavy fire; Mantis demolishes a bot with a low-blow uppercut while AM downloads the drone’s core data.

  • 24-7 and Escape:
    Mantis Shrimp distracts 24-7, earning his autograph on the ion pack before shrinking and diving into the river.
    Automatic Man gains limited control of the drone and flies it off as Manifester projects a false pursuit illusion.

  • Mission Complete (Almost):
    Manifester teleports to the getaway supercar as Templar rappels inside. They speed away under the bridge, leaving Mantis submerged and the mission—barely—successful.

Heroes

  • Mantis Shrimp – Undercover infiltration and crowd control; defeats Platypus and recovers data under heavy fire.
  • Manifester – Provides illusion cover and misdirection (despite several critical failures).
  • Templar – Precision shooter neutralizing multiple bots and monitoring incoming threats.
  • Automatic Man – Hacks the drone successfully, extracting critical intel for MM2.

Villains and Threats

  • Madman #1 – Offscreen, the original “asshole” version aiding Greitens.
  • Madman #2
  • ActiVADER – Captured anomaly used as leverage.
  • Boba Fett – Sedated and detained by MM2.
  • Platypus – Cryptid attacker from Dimension 24.
  • 24-7 – Arrives mid-mission, complicating extraction.
  • Khaki Tacs and Drone Security Bots – Station defense forces guarding the ionic recharging array.

Other Characters

  • Oscar – MM2’s assistant and comic relief; loves grilled bologna.
  • Eric Greitens – Still operating behind the scenes, coordinating with Madman #1.
  • Heart of America Bridge Technicians – Human overseers of the drone maintenance system.
  • Platypus – Anomaly reference from U24; neutralized in combat.

Notes

  • Madman #2 provides crucial context for the interdimensional conflict and identifies Greitens’s plan to weaponize multiple Automatic Men.
  • This mission confirms that Dimension 1 is surveilled with advanced ionic drone technology connected to Eric Greitens’ regime.
  • The team narrowly completes the data capture, setting up the eventual assault on MM1’s compound.
  • Multiple critical failures by Manifester inject humor and chaos into an otherwise tense infiltration.

Raw Session Notes

6/9/24 Session 16

After lowering weapon, Madman invites Manifester, Templar and Mantis into his workshop. (AM “sleeping” in a car elsewhere.)
Workshop is rustic (~1112 Gentry St, N. Kansas City, MO). Inside gritty. Small childs toy starts up as a warning as we enter, Madman turns off. Man in late 40s with thinning hair, blaster over shoulder—Oscar—at a grill, offers grilled bologna sandwiches, coffee, large orange cooler full of lemonade.
“We should deal with prisoners. We need Activator, but not Bobba Fett. Can’t have Bobba giving away my workshop location.” Injects him with something that knocks him out. Oscar leads a groggy Activator to a side room with chain attached to floor. Activator wakes enough to say “Manifester, I am your…” then door closes.
MM “Ok let’s talk.” Goes to a whiteboard. Writes “D1.” “Look we’re in D1. You’re from D24. You are interdimensional bad asses. I am from Dimension 2.” He has roman numeral 2 tattooed on his forearm. Greitens added the tattoo.
MM2 “In Dimension 2, I invented a machine that… think of dimensions like floors on a building, and I invented an elevator. It’s not perfect, but it is very powerful. Where I’m from, Greitens was Trump’s vice president. Once he got in the position, he staged a coup, took over the government and declared himself emperor of north America. I was using my tech to fight Greitens, but one day I got sucked into this dimension.”
MM2 “It turns out the me here, Madman #1, is a serious asshole. Madman #1 and Greitens #1 are working together. They pulled me here to prevent Greitens #2 from getting my technology. Here they force me to work with Greitens. MM here is better at some things than I am. MM 1 can pull people into a dimension that he’s in. I can only swap people with their alternate selves. But something I can do that asshole me can’t do, or at least not as well, is get information. I can read information from a dimension like a leaf falling on a river. That’s what they use me for. That’s how I determined their plan and how I found you; because you defeated Greitens in your dimension.”
Templar hears Automatic Man falling from sky from superleap. “Friend incoming.”
MM2 “There plan is to get 4-5 dozen automatic mans here, enslave them using technology, and use them to take over the world.”
We hear AM crashing into street outside. Oscar “Speak of the devil! Want any fried bologna?”
MM2 “So I need your help. The dimension needs your help. I don’t want to go into all of this. I need your help on an assault on asshole-me’s compound. But before we do that, there are some steps we need to take.” He returns to whiteboard. “The city is surveyed in many ways by authorities. One of the ways is with drones powered by ionic power with recharging station near Heart of America Bridge. We have surveillance of how this operates and when. Drones come in from the south. System brings them in exactly so wings end up on conveyor belt, pull them through, and they are recharged. We want to obtain software out of one of the drones. Asshole-me’s compound is surrounded by lots of bots. We can’t just capture one bc they have a self-destruct system, but this is deactivated during charging.”
Drones build like stingrays; wider than long. 30 ft across by 10-15 ft long. There’s some level of security with bot technicians and 1-2 human technicians ensuring no problems with machines. On top of these drones, there’s a maintenance hatch with flip-top opening to reveal punch-pad; code opens hatch. He has a flash drive that we can stick into port, wait a few seconds, then remove and bring back to MM2. MM2 knows passcodes but doesn’t know what drone we have until we have it. We’ll need to get ID number off the drone to determine which code to use. Number is right by the maintenance hatch.
MM2 notes to Manifester that drones/bots likely not tricked by illusions.
Charging takes 2-3 minutes.
Mantis floats in on trout blimp, shrunken, then drops onto the top of the drone charging trench. Automatic Man jumps from Heart of America Bridge to the other end. Templar sets up on the peak of the Kit Bond bridge nearby, wearing white camo to blend in. Manifester fishes from the pedestrian bridge; crafts illusion of setting a massive, record-setting fish—wearing Darrell Waltrip t-shirt, caterpillar trucker cap.
We see at least 4 non-human bots with blaster rifles; at least 2 Khaki Tacts, but we suspect there are more. A drone comes in from the south, banks to enter the system.
Manifester waves to the KTs, then attempts his illusion of giant fish, fails and hooks a bra instead. “You ever see one this big?” he shouts to the guards and onlookers.
AM can hear radio chatter, guards note blimp but they aren’t suspicious.
Mantis drops atop the tower, but a crow grabs him then drops him over the river. Mantis falls into the river. Commlink is down. He had to unshrink to swim his way to the middle post in the river.
Automatic Man jumps from Berkley side to the top of the conveyor system as the drone enters. AM lands exactly as planned, but it is loud and an alarm goes off. “Intruder, intruder, intruder…” and someone opens a door just beside him. AM closes the door with extreme prejudice. In front of him, there’s the drone; a catwalk just above the drone, then a level that the bots are on.
Initiative: Todd, Eric, Brett, Matt
Templar can see 4 bots getting into position to take shots at AM with energy weapons. Templar uses ricochet shot to hit 4 of them. Two bots collapse into sparks, two bots stagger but not destroyed.
As Mantis pulls himself out of the water, a beaked and human-headed creature pokes his head up and hisses. Mantis recognizes Platypus, an evil anomaly (cryptid) in D24. He springs at Mantis and tries to bite but hits armor instead.
Manifester mind blasts Platypus for 13 dmg. Platypus freezes up and collapses back into the river.
Automatic Man drops on top of the drone. He locates the hatch and serial number. He alerts everyone to the serial number on the coms. Madman2 and Oscar radio back, “01 27 14.”
Mantis jet pack flies onto the drone across from Automatic Man. More bots arrive above us; still two bots ahead of us.
Templar sees a person flying towards us—recognizes them as 24-7—coming over the river from the west. Templar alerts us by radio. Madman2 says “oh no, 24-7?” Oscar starts singing 24-7’s theme. Madman2 “Oscar, shut up!” Templar shoots the two bots as they close down on Mantis and Automatic Man, they go down.
Manifester attempts to create illusion just for 24-7 that bridge, charging station, and everyone on it blinks out of existence… natural 1. Instead Mantis Shrimp and Automatic Man now have little arrows over their head pointing them out to 24-7.
Four bots on catwalk level fire down on Mantis and AM. 2 miss, 2 hit with electricity. Mantis 5 dmg. AM repels the hit.
Automatic Man puts in the code. It beeps three times, then doesn’t open. “Oscar your handwriting is terrible. It’s 01-27-19.” AM types it in; hatch opens. AM puts his thumb into thumb drive; he feels tingling as data enters his thumb.
Mantis jets up to catwalk, punches nearest bot in the junk so hard it’s legs fall off. Automatic Man videos it for later use on a billboard.
Templar broadcasts on broad channel “24-7, stand down.” Rolls a 4.
Manifester re-attempts illusion, this time for the Khaki Tacts. Rolls a 1. Now the Khaki Tacts see an arrow pointing over Manifester’s head.
Automatic Man still has thumb in drive, but uses free hand blaster to shoot at bot. Misses.
Bots shoot: 1 misses Mantis, 1 hits AM but shield protects him, 1 misses AM entirely.
Mantis jets over towards 24-7 and asks for his autograph. Succeeds on roll! 24-7 signs ion pack using ink that Mantis secretes. “I’ll sign, but you have to come with me, and I have to apprehend the criminals on the charging station. I saw energy blasts!”
A heavily armed bot appears; Templar shoots it and it staggers.
Manifester blinks up to highway away from Khaki Tacts.
Automatic Man downloads the drone data; nat 20, can control drone now. He flies off on the drone.
Manifester makes illusion for 24-7 that Automatic Man flies off on drone in the wrong direction. Manifester then blinks again to location of super vehicle. He drives down highway just below Templar who drops down a rope into the car.
Mantis distracts 24-7 as long as possible, then shrinks and cuts out his ion pack, falling into the river.
