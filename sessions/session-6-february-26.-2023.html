Key Events:

  • Further Confrontations: After a tense exchange at the hotel, Mantis Shrimp interrogates the captured M.O.L.E. while Automatic Man 2.0 and the others confront The Master about his manipulations involving the city council and media. Barbara, a key witness and victim of The Master’s manipulations, urges the group to keep a close eye on him, revealing his limitation of controlling only one mind at a time.
  • Gnome and CRICKET: Automatic Man 2.0 uncovers his past connections with The Gnome and the CRICKET organization, which aims to preserve human knowledge against potential cataclysms. Tensions arise from past conflicts and personal entanglements within this group.
  • Negotiations and Deceptions: The team manipulates The Master into making a phone call to The M.O.L.E.S., arranging the release of a kidnapped councilman as a strategic move to alleviate tensions with Epsilon Force. They also plan to remove a false memory implanted in a reporter at a concert as a demonstration of good faith.

Note: This session intertwines complex character interactions with strategic maneuvers, revealing deeper layers of the narrative involving political manipulation, supernatural abilities, and secretive organizations. The characters’ actions reflect a blend of confrontation, negotiation, and investigative tactics as they navigate the challenges posed by their adversaries and the mysterious circumstances surrounding them.
