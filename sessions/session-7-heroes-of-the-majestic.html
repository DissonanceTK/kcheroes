Session 7 - Heroes of The Majestic

Session Summary

In-Game Date: April 11, 2022
Dimension: 24

Key Events

  • Lies and Logistics:
    Manifester spins an elaborate story to Leandra, claiming his Celica was towed as abandoned while he was at QuikTrip buying a taquito, preventing him from meeting her at John’s Big Deck.

  • The Investigation Begins:
    The team—Templar, Mantis Shrimp, Automatic Man, and Manifester—regroups at the Bossanova Hotel and drives to Briarcliff to investigate The Gnome’s location and the rumored geodesic dome facility nearby.

  • Suspicious Containers:
    They find shipping containers near The Gnome’s supposed apartment.

    • Templar scopes the area, spotting only boxes and ordinary items, but notices a powered refrigeration unit labeled in Korean: “Wuhon Institute of Virology.”
    • Mantis Shrimp pries open a container, discovering neatly arranged cases, a small fridge with vials of glowing green fluid (smelling of acidic lime). He pockets one vial.
    • The group finds no documents but several energy weapons. Mantis keeps one small weapon, giving two others (large and small) to Templar.

  • Police Arrival:
    Automatic Man intercepts a 911 call reporting the disturbance, even identifying the caller’s number. Mantis, feigning civic duty, calls 911 himself to “report a suspicious shipping container.”
    He sits casually atop the opened container, chatting on the phone as two police cars screech in. Officer Michaels recognizes him: “Mantis Shrimp!” After inspecting the scene, they cuff and arrest him.

    • Mantis slips free of one cuff, calls anomaly lawyer Paul Hood, and theatrically declares, “On advice of my lawyer, I must bail.” He rips open the car door and escapes, arranging to meet the team at Mimi’s Pantry.

  • Unknown Caller:
    When they regroup, Automatic Man’s satellite phone rings. A cryptic voice says:

    “Did you get the paper? Did you get The Star? … The Spring Training will be illuminating, don’t you think?”
    The line goes dead.

  • Men in Black Surveillance:
    Templar senses unease—too many open sightlines near the dome. Through his scope, he spots two observers in a black car: an Asian man and an older “Abe Vigoda–looking” companion.
    Manifester creates an illusion making the older man believe he’s being swarmed by ants, causing him real distress and distracting his partner. The group dashes to the dome.

  • Inside the Geodesic Dome:
    As they approach, Templar’s x-ray vision fails to penetrate the structure, and Manifester notes its alien-like texture—reminiscent of Roswell rumors.
    A camera above the entrance tracks Automatic Man. When he steps forward, the door opens automatically. Inside, the space resembles a lush greenhouse, filled with fruit-bearing plants tended by boxy robots. The door seals behind them, cutting off cell service.
    A voice from a speaker greets them:

    “Did you see the second Royals game last Tuesday? That game was a lot of fun.”
    AM checks the box score—there was indeed a mysterious second game recorded as 10–10. When he speaks the score aloud, the room rotates, and a floor drain opens to reveal a spiral staircase descending below.

  • Descent into the Facility:
    They descend into a square chamber (12x12 ft) lined with downward-facing spigots. A robot on tracks greets them:

    “Four visitors.”
    A bulkhead door slides aside, revealing another chamber and a robot labeled “Twelve.” It recognizes “Mr. Von Neuman” (Automatic Man) and welcomes him.

    A second bulkhead opens into a vast hallway filled with robots, monitors, and advanced machinery. One screen plays Buck Rogers, another an old India vs. West Indies cricket match.

  • Dr. Goodfellow:
    A humanoid robot introduces himself as Dr. Goodfellow, greeting Automatic Man warmly:

    “When the Gnome and the Fiddler had their battle, I fooled their sensors into thinking this facility was destroyed.”
    Manifester quips, “Technically that was an illusion. A trick is what a whore does for money.”

    Dr. G explains that his memories of their disagreement were deleted, but he recalls it involved a dispute of method—The Gnome sought action, The Fiddler preferred observation.

  • The Inverted Mayan Long Calendar:
    Dr. Goodfellow now works on the “Inverted Mayan Long Calendar,” an analytical model predicting future crises. He invites the team to “hear a bit of the future,” combining random inputs into a bizarre prophecy:

    “In the next 3–5 days, a very significant event involving 317.576 trustworthy, stationary trout.”
    Automatic Man senses the entire structure is akin to a land-based submarine.

  • Revelations About the Past:

    • Automatic Man learns he was built by The Fiddler.
    • Dr. Goodfellow explains:
      • The Fiddler is a human male, 6'1.25", 184 lbs, committed to human survival.
      • The Gnome and the Fiddler’s rivalry endangered CRICKET’s goals.
      • Automatic Man was created as an envoy—the intermediary between humans and future AI.
      • The bots have grown eccentric, fixating on cricket and Buck Rogers.
    • When Mantis Shrimp asks how Fiddler would view him, Dr. G replies coldly:

      “Fiddler would not see you as human.”

Heroes

  • Automatic Man – Traces more of his origin; recognized as “Mr. Von Neuman” by the dome’s AI network.
  • Templar – Provides overwatch, spots surveillance, and observes alien technology.
  • Mantis Shrimp – Breaks into containers, steals samples and energy weapons, arrested and escapes dramatically.
  • Manifester – Employs illusions and wit to distract enemies and assist infiltration; provides comic counterpoint.

Supporting Characters

  • Leandra – Briefly contacted by Manifester; unaware of the larger mission.
  • Mark Renallo – Not present but still tied to the Gnome plotline.
  • Dr. Goodfellow – Robotic scientist, caretaker of the dome and researcher of predictive futures.
  • The Fiddler – Revealed as Automatic Man’s creator and former CRICKET leader.
  • The Gnome – Mentioned as Fiddler’s counterpart in the CRICKET schism.
  • Officer Michaels – Arrests Mantis before his comedic escape.
  • Abe Vigoda-type agent & partner – Men in Black surveillance team.

Notes

This session reveals Automatic Man’s true creator (The Fiddler) and the nature of the CRICKET conflict.
Dr. Goodfellow’s introduction expands the mythos of anomaly-linked AI and predictive systems.
Humor, tension, and philosophy collide—blending noir absurdity with revelations about free will, machine evolution, and human survival.
