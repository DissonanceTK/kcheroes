Session 9 - It's All a Blur

Session Summary

In-Game Date: April 11, 2022
Dimension: 1

Key Events

  • A Familiar Enemy Returns:
    A local hero known as Blurry reports prior battles against Meat Shield, Mosh Pit, and Fire Cube, including a previous team-up with Templar in a church.
    This is the second time he’s killed Mosh Pit, but in earlier encounters, killing Mosh Pit caused people under his trance to fall into comas—some still remain comatose.

  • Incoming Alert:
    Cell phones across the team buzz with the cryptic message: “Blurry 51-50 H H.”
    Blurry contacts them, explaining that the comatose victims are being moved. He insists they must intervene despite warnings from his AI assistant, Jerry.

  • The Supercar Unveiled:
    The group meets Blurry at a garage, where he proudly reveals Templar’s upgraded stealth Humvee—a combat-ready vehicle with retractable turret and an onboard AI.

    • Jerry disapproves of the rescue mission.
    • Blurry believes Manifester can wake the coma victims through psychic means.
    • Jerry detects three tactical troop carriers, warning that two are likely decoys.

  • Highway Pursuit:
    In Templar’s Humvee, the heroes race after the transports.

    • Blurry warns of a sniper on an overpass.
    • Templar fires but misses; Manifester creates an illusion of the Humvee being slightly offset, tricking the enemy’s aim.
    • The illusion of the car exploding convinces their attackers they’ve been destroyed.

  • Engagement with the Convoy:
    Templar closes in on the trucks.

    • Mantis Shrimp leaps onto the first vehicle’s roof as Khaki Tacs open fire.
    • Manifester uses a psychic blast—described as a “heat wave”—knocking one gunman unconscious.
    • Templar commands the drivers over the PA: “Stop!”
    • The trucks brake suddenly—throwing Mantis Shrimp off and sending him tumbling along the highway.
    • Templar exits the Humvee, guns drawn, as soldiers emerge and return fire.

  • Coordinated Combat:

    • Manifester leaps from the car with theatrical mist and a grand gesture, attempting to conjure fire raining from the sky—fails, then hides behind the vehicle.
    • Over comms, Blurry declares: “I’m about to go out of focus. I’m starting with the middle one.”
    • Moments later, the middle truck phases, dropping its occupants onto the pavement.
    • Blurry yells: “It’s not number 2!”
    • Templar disables the fleeing third truck’s tire, slowing it down.
    • Mantis Shrimp attempts to leap aboard but falls short—getting run over in the process.
    • Templar rolls under the truck for cover, ricochets a bullet that pops another tire and incapacitates two Khaki Tacs.
    • Mantis rips off the bumper in frustration.
    • Manifester casts an illusion of the truck on fire, scattering the enemy troops.

  • Rescue:
    Templar opens the truck’s rear compartment, revealing three unconscious people strapped to medical gurneys with monitors attached.

    • Manifester creates a calming mass illusion of quiet, which wakes them from their comas—confused but alive.
    • The group loads them into Templar’s Humvee and escapes the scene.
    • Jerry, surprised the plan actually succeeded, mutters his disbelief.

  • Safehouse and the Resistance:
    Blurry directs them to his safehouse at 1580 E 19th Street, disguised as an autobody shop.
    Jerry quickly realizes this Templar is not from their dimension, identifying the heroes as off-worlders.

    They meet members of a resistance movement opposing a totalitarian Kansas City government:

    • Cash is outlawed, replaced entirely by digital credits.
    • FEMA and surveillance networks monitor all citizen movement.
    • KC is effectively partitioned off from the rest of the U.S.

  • Unexpected Call:
    Manifester’s phone rings. The caller ID reads: “The Wife.”
    He looks at it and says:

    “I’m not going to pick that up.”

Heroes

  • Templar – Takes command in tactical combat, rescues captives, and navigates interdimensional confusion.
  • Mantis Shrimp – Engages trucks physically, endures multiple impacts, and adds comic resilience.
  • Larry Harry Jones – Uses illusion and psychic force to control chaos, freeing coma victims.
  • Automatic Man – Provides coordination, communications, and situational awareness (background role).
  • Blurry – New ally; can phase through matter, disrupt vehicles, and has ties to a local resistance.

Supporting Characters

  • Jerry – AI companion in Templar’s Humvee; pragmatic, dryly skeptical.
  • Khaki Tacs – Armed paramilitary unit escorting comatose citizens.
  • The Resistance – Underground movement opposing KC’s authoritarian rule in Dimension 1.
  • The Wife – Identity unknown; contacts Manifester at session’s end.

Notes

This session expands the Dimension 1 timeline, revealing a dystopian Kansas City under martial surveillance and digital currency control.
Blurry introduces local stakes and history with familiar villains, suggesting dimensional echoes of earlier foes.
The tone blends cinematic vehicular action with dark satire—“Jerry thinks it’s a bad idea” versus “I’m not going to pick that up.”
The session closes on unease, resistance intrigue, and the unsettling realization that the heroes may not be able to return home easily.
