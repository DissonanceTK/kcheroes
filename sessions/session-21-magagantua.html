Session 21 - MAGAgantua

Session Summary

In-Game Date:
Dimension: 1

Key Events

  • Nazka Descends:
    A 9-foot being with desert-stone regalia and pupil-less yellow eyes emerges from the elevator. Identified by Madman Markham #1 as Nazka, he declares, “Only stone endures,” and begins rust-aging the catwalk under Templar. Nazka challenges Templar directly as a “worthy opponent.”

  • Battle on Two Fronts:
    Outside, Automatic Man trades fire with AM XXX; inside, Nazka’s decay effect spreads as Madman #1 begs to be freed. Manifester sprints back through the breached wall shouting that the Teslasphere will explode.

  • Doom Calculus:
    Over comms, Madman #2 and Oscar warn that if the rolling Teslasphere hits an Ion Tower, a negation dome (~300 yards) could erase everything. Priority shifts to stopping or outrunning the sphere.

  • Chaos Among Civilians:
    Shrapnel from the destroyed firetruck ignites a Hazmat Local 83 shed; four suited workers flee—into the Teslasphere’s path.

  • Greitens Rises (Again):
    At Nazka’s “Arise!” command, Eric Greitens stirs atop the dimensional tube, wielding a shield and laser sickle as Centurian American. Templar’s explosive round partially blocked by a stone monolith still staggers Nazka.

  • Crossfire and Collapses:
    AM XXX opens up “full auto,” wounding an alien tech and a hazmat worker; Mantis Shrimp batters XXX’s armor; Automatic Man and Templar tag Eric Greitens, who totters between dead and barely alive. The hangar corrodes and collapses into rust; fissures open underfoot.

  • Countdown to Impact:
    With 4→3→2 rounds to impact, Mantis evacuates hazmat workers; Blurry returns and boosts Templar with tonic (SP restore). Nazka drops Manifester and Blurry into fissures; Eric Greitens yo-yos between “down” and “up.” Automatic Man and XXX slug it out; armor finally shreds.

  • Portals and Ricochets:
    Manifester opens a parking-lot portal; Templar’s ricochet chain shot drops XXX, clips Nazka hard, and re-drops Eric Greitens. Nazka calls Templar a “worthy foe” as fissures begin to close.

  • Last-Second Evac:
    With 1 round left, Mantis grabs the wounded alien and the control device; Blurry fireman-carries hazmat workers; Manifester, Automatic Man (yanked through by Blurry), and Templar all dive through the portal under fire.

  • Silent Light:
    AM XXIII leaps away. The Teslasphere contacts the Ion Tower—a silent, expanding flash nearly reaches the evac point. After the brilliance fades…

  • Hard Cut — New Room, New Universe:
    Vision returns. The team (Blurry, Mantis Shrimp, Automatic Man, Manifester—notably without Templar in view) sits on fancy furniture in a strange “clubhouse.” Portraits of Al Capone and Tom Pendergast loom behind the actual men they represent; a red-haired woman in a lab coat with horn-rims smiles. Capone looks at the redhead and quips, “Eh, dollface, it worked!”

Heroes

  • Automatic Man — Trades fire with AM XXX; gauges AM control states; helps anchor the retreat; nearly last through the portal.
  • Templar — Survives collapsing catwalk; ricochet chains into Greitens, Nazka, and XXX; rolling jump through portal under pressure.
  • Mantis Shrimp — Evacs civilians; pummels AM XXX; extracts wounded alien and device; leads “everyone through the portal!”
  • Manifester — Battlefield routing; creates escape portal to the parking lot; attempts (near-success) illusion on Nazka during the melee.
  • Blurry — Rescues Vicky Diez Camacho (prior session); SP tonic assist to Templar; yanks AM through portal; carries hazmat workers to safety.

Villains and Threats

  • Nazka — Stone-themed entity; induces rapid rust/decay, raises monolith shields, opens/ closes fissures; targets “sorcerer” (Manifester) and duels Templar.
  • Eric Greitens / Centurian American — Reanimated by Nazka; shield + laser sickle; repeatedly dropped by gunfire; fate uncertain after the flash.
  • AM XXX — Heavily armored, minigun variant; “full auto” hazards; ultimately destroyed by focused fire.
  • AM XXIII — Identical to AM XXIV; disengages via super jump before the detonation.
  • Teslasphere + Ion Tower — Rolling energy sphere triggers silent negation-flash on contact; large-area reality threat.

Other Characters

  • Madman Markham #1 — Chained, panicked; survives to the portal moment.
    • Madman Markham #2 & Oscar — Remote advisors performing high-risk estimates.
  • Alien Technicians (Grays) — One killed by AM XXX; one badly wounded and extracted by Mantis with a control device.
  • Vicky Diez Camacho — Offsite after Blurry’s earlier rescue; asks to confirm Greitens’ death over comms.
  • Hazmat Local 83 Workers — Four civilians caught in the chaos; three visibly evac’d through the portal.

Notes

  • Nazka Mechanics (at table): Raises stone monoliths; global rust/decay aura; terrain fissures; taunts “sorcerer.”
  • Control States: AM variants no longer under broadcast control post-disruption; AM XXX behaves as autonomous war-bot.
  • Countdown Clock: Explicit round tracker to Teslasphere–Ion Tower impact drove scene pacing and evacuation priorities.
  • Stinger: Post-flash cut to “clubhouse” with Capone/Pendergast portraits and a red-haired lab tech; Templar’s immediate status/location unclear.
