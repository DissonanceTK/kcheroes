Session 13 - Fifth and Delphi

Session Summary

In-Game Date:
Dimension: 1

Key Events

  • Aftermath at the Club:
    Sammy Von Newman collects biological samples from the fallen Master of the Hunt.

  • Leandra & Manifester:
    Manifester learns he and Leandra are professional thieves/con artists in this universe; he also preaches, and followers often report “visions” of his sermons.

  • Dimension-1 Civic Climate:
    Cash is being phased out for a government debit-card system. Leandra is trying to steal the code tied to this system to create a never-ending debit card.

  • Templar’s Reputation & Lead:
    In this timeline, the Cathedral of the Immaculate Conception suffered heavy damage; a priest died and Templar is blamed (possibly due to Meat Shield). Templar meets Marvin, who points him to a soup kitchen/shelter and later to Grand Avenue Temple.

  • Notes & Hooks from “Dave” (The Master):
    “Dave” provides notes on The Gnome and The Fiddler, plus leads:

    • New A.R.I.E.S. project to be run at Hangar 50.
    • Natural Philosophers expected at Pecks Building, 1099 Baltimore Ave.
    • Reference to a hidden depot disguised as a fireworks stand; rumor of N. Korean invasion; questions why the Fiddler needs so much power.
    • Personal aside: he feels kinship with Mantis, but is terrified of “your friend with the gun.”

  • Claude’s Dojo subplot:
    Mantis Shrimp maintains a dojo in River Market; rivalry set up for a public showdown with Hipster Deadpool’s dojo. Jimbo Shrump assists.

  • Forensics Thread:
    Prior DNA testing on a sulfur-based “monster” shows no DNA for the creature; collateral DNA points to potential relatives Lee & Sara McClauren (MoDOC) and Tom McClauren (a felon) — **Tom is visibly Jerry.

  • Grand Avenue Temple — Delphi:
    Guided by Marvin, the team meets Delphi (paper-stacked office, lemonade/carrots). He says Councilman Eric Bunch remains jailed despite the reporter’s recantation and urges a covert interview. He supplies David Bell’s photo (Bunch’s attorney), jail floorplans, and entry procedure. Payment offered: $10k (Templar) / $7.5k (Mantis).

  • Jail Infiltration:
    Disguises: Manifester as Andy Reid; Mantis with a Don Johnson mullet; Templar (unspecified).
    They pass the metal detectors with tiny/small/large energy guns and reach 5th floor.
    Eric Bunch explains his automatic writing power: at an ionic power-plant meeting he compulsively recorded details about an out-of-place attendee — The Fiddler — including the machine schematic and motive. He hid the notes at home; he now senses they’re in Sen. Josh Hawley’s Western MO office.

  • Alarm & Escape:
    When David Bell arrives, Manifester’s illusion stutters (for a second they all look like Richard Nixon). Guard alerts trigger, so Manifester casts a jail-on-fire illusion and Templar pulls the fire alarm.
    During the evac, Manifester’s disguise turns the team into Village People archetypes. They ultimately leave Bunch behind (reluctant to flee).
    Outside, Automatic Man is disguised as Bunch; they swap in the commotion and head for Hawley’s office.

  • Office Breach (Hawley & Greitens):
    At the building, front desk refuses access. Manifester twice portals — first misplacing it outside, then successfully into the office, where Josh Hawley and Eric Greitens are present.
    Combat:

    • Hawley grabs a shotgun and fires (miss).
    • Greitens seizes a Civil War sword and strikes Mantis (2 dmg).
    • Mantis flips Hawley’s desk, then smashes the shotgun away.
    • Templar zipties Hawley.
    • Manifester mind blasts Greitens (down).
    • A floor safe is revealed; two armored guards enter but retreat when Templar commands.
    • Mantis rips open the safe: mummified hand, handgun, simple dagger, four gold bars, laminated papers.
      The team portals out (after a misfire to 60 floors up) disguised as cleaning staff.

  • Return & Reset:
    At the jail, an exterior hole ~5 floors up lets them slip Bunch back inside (Mantis carries him up while still “Village People” disguised).
    Findings: Notes identify Fiddler = Mike “Madman” Markham; his device contacts other dimensions to snag superbeings and bring them here. Items from the safe are secured.
