Session 14 – Enter the Shrimp

Session Summary

In-Game Date:
Dimension: 1

Key Events

  • A Spring Showdown:
    A week after the events at Senator Hawley’s office, Kansas City enjoys a calm spring day — until the Inter-Dojo Tournament begins at the Builder’s Steel facility, organized by Jimbo Shrump with permission from Mark Renallo.

  • The Next Up Challenge:
    The event is hosted by Big Bingo, catered by Captain Happy’s Catfish House (with the fish blimp), and awards a ceremonial katana plus a briefcase of cash to the winner.

  • Competitors:
    Entrants include Mantis Shrimp, Hipster Deadpool, and other martial artists: Cranekick, Nunchucks, Giraffe, and Blue Ninja (later revealed to be Ghost Dancer).

  • Anomalies in the Crowd:
    Among the spectators are Rainbow and the rowdy rugby crew The Union (members 8 Man, Scrum Half, and Chubby Chaser).

  • Imperial Interruption:
    A Doritos truck arrives blasting the Imperial March; six faux Stormtroopers and anomaly ActiVADER (a power manipulator) emerge, soon joined by a faux Boba Fett who attacks a police helicopter.

  • Early Chaos:
    Mantis Shrimp accidentally kills — then immediately witnesses the regeneration of — Hipster Deadpool, while Templar and Manifester engage the invading anomalies.

  • Precision & Pandemonium:
    Templar’s ricochet shots disable most Stormtroopers; Manifester uses illusions to confuse foes (and inadvertently terrifies Big Bingo and Jimbo Shrump with “rats”).

  • Heroic Save:
    Mantis Shrimp redirects a crashing police helicopter into a bounce house, saving civilians but taking injuries.

  • Jetpack Down:
    Templar shoots Boba Fett’s jetpack, sending him crashing; meanwhile The Union sprints off with the prize money.

  • Blue Ninja Revealed:
    Ghost Dancer (posing as Blue Ninja) attacks Mantis Shrimp but is defeated by a devastating uppercut.

  • Bingo’s Verdict:
    Big Bingo declares it “the best interdojo tournament north or south of the river!”

  • Aftermath — The Fiddler Appears:
    Investigating the crash site, the heroes find “Vader” and Boba Fett downed — and confront Madman Markham, who says, “I am not the Madman that you seek.”

Heroes

  • Mantis Shrimp – Tournament participant; rescues civilians and defeats Ghost Dancer.
  • Manifester – Uses illusions during the chaotic battle; accidentally causes mass hysteria over imaginary rats.
  • Templar – Provides tactical precision, neutralizing multiple threats with ricochet shots and disabling ActiVADER and Boba Fett.

Villains and Threats

  • ActiVADER – An anomaly who manipulates the powers of others; leads the Stormtrooper squad.
  • Ghost Dancer – Appears under the guise of Blue Ninja; defeated by Mantis.
  • Boba Fett – Attacks a police helicopter and later crashes.
  • The Union – Local rugby team who seizes the prize money amid the chaos.

Other Characters

  • Jimbo Shrump – Helps organize the tournament; panics during Manifester’s illusion.
  • Big Bingo – Charismatic MC of the event.
  • Hipster Deadpool – Regenerating fighter and Mantis’s dojo rival.
  • Mark Renallo – Provides permission for the tournament venue.
  • Madman Markham – Appears at the end, claiming to be from a different reality.

Notes

  • This session blends comic absurdity with public spectacle, marking a lighter interlude before the next confrontation with The Fiddler.
  • The crowd’s response and the escalation of anomaly appearances suggest increased instability in this dimension’s Kansas City.

4/20/24 Session 14
It’s the next Saturday. A nice sunny spring day, not too hot.
Since that event, we discovered that Josh Hawley was in two places at once (also in DC doing Senate hearings while we were fighting him). Eric Bunch is scheduled to have hearing to modify his bond. Rumor on street is Meatshield is trying to hire people to kill one or all of us.
But TODAY is the Inter-Dojo Tournament!
Jimbo managed to secure a location other than the dojos for the event because these can get out of hand at the Builder’s Steel facility. Got permission from Mark Renallo. (Unknown if this Renallo has interacted with Mantis)
This is the Next Up Challenge with Big Bingo (the MC and the event after the dojo). Held regularly. Catered by the Captain Happy’s Catfish house; the fish blimp is there. There is a trophy that is a ceremonial katana that goes to the winner. Also by sword is a briefcase of prize money.
Big Bingo tall portly black man with glasses, white suit with bingo numbers.
The events:

  1. Kada demonstration on a flatbed truck
  2. Long steel girder for pugilstick battle atop girder
  3. ?????????? Next Up challengers

    Next Up Challengers: Cranekick (does lots of Karate Kid crane kicks), Nunchucks (can copy himself), Giraffe (stretchy neck, headbutts people), and Blue Ninja (is ninja) are the other competitors who will battle for honor of fighting winner of Mantis vs. Deadpool.
    Hipster Deadpool rolls up in his vespa. Lots of trash talk before event. He’s dressed like Deadpool in movies but has mustache sticking through mask and a blackbelt.
    In addition to crowd of normies; a few other anomalies show up: Rainbow, tall woman with skintight rainbow body suit flared at ankles and wrists, carries bow staff; The Union, several somewhat drunk obnoxious dudes in rugby shirts (8 Man, Scrum Half, and Chubby Chaser #2).

Six people in stormtrooper outfits get out and a tall guy dressed like Darth Vader except a white t-shirt with “ActiVADER” written on it. He is anomaly with ability to mess with how other people’s powers work. Storm troopers have energy-looking blasters. Crowd starts grumbling at them.

    3, 11, 10, 11, 7, 9, 7 vader – all but 2 stormtroopers and vader collapse
    Vader attacks Manifester; one of Manifesters portals opens high above him but he does not fall through it
    Mantis attempts to jetpack punch towards Vader, but a stormtrooper jumps in the way. Mantis catches them and throws the stormtrooper at the table with the money and katana so that they bounce to Jimbo and Bingo.
    Manifester tries to mind blast Vader but almost blasts Mantis instead.
    Police helicopter arrives and says “this is an unlawful anomaly assembly. Disperse at once or be arrested” Out of black van someone in a bobafet outfit comes out and shoots the helicopter. It begins crashing towards the fish blimp
    Nunchucks divided himself in half and is running towards Big Bingo; unclear if helping or headed for money.

    The Giraffe runs towards the blimp and tries to use his neck to grab it.

    ActiVADER attacks Mantis. He transforms back into Claude Crevette from the waist down.
