Session 12 - First Laws at the Majestic

Session Summary

In-Game Date: April 11, 2022
Dimension: 1

Key Events

  • Back at the Hotel:
    The captive The Master now appears human (resembling actor William Smith). Mantis Shrimp abruptly shifts into his baseline identity Claude Crevette, losing most powers. The Master is rattled and fears he won’t survive the night.

  • “Dave” the Master:
    Larry Harry Jones successfully implants the belief that the Master’s name is “Dave.” “Dave” bargains for release after he fixes Vicki’s memories and provides a **write-up on The Gnome.

  • Disguises Go Awry:
    Manifester’s illusion disguises accidentally make the team look like Macho Man Randy Savage clones.

  • At The Majestic:
    In the crowded, KT-heavy club, Maxwell Manning rails against opening for a Bon Jovi cover band, The Ardent Natural Philosophers of BJ.
    Off-duty Khaki Tacs drink and mourn two colleagues killed earlier. Templar learns they expect a coming “advantage” (supersoldier serum; internal comms), gleaned by “Dave” via mind read.

  • Leandra Sighting:
    Leandra is present in a red cocktail dress; private watch/text exchange with Manifester confirms covert coordination.

  • Opening Act — Dr. Fervid & Co.:
    Dr. Fervid (frontman) introduces Master of the Hunt (guitar/axe), Hannah the Hammer (drums/dulcimer), The Banshees (backup singers), and Sammy Von Newman (AM look-alike on bass). They launch into “Bad Medicine.”

  • Vicki’s Memory Fix:
    Downstairs, “Dave” removes Vicki’s implanted false memory (2–3 minutes, traumatic). She collapses sobbing; Automatic Man escorts her back to friend Dianne.

  • MOLES Breach / Music Control:
    A M.O.L.E.S. darkness mass bursts through a wall. The band’s enchantment freezes civilians with a looping “Livin’ on a Prayer” chorus, and Banshees push KTs toward violence.

  • Countermoves:
    Templar shoots the amp power—music softens. He threatens the band.
    AM’s blast craters a wall (no injuries). Dr. Fervid eye-blasts at Templar.
    Manifester mutes the singers with illusion, snapping KTs out of it; the Banshees notice the interference.
    Automatic Man and Maxwell spark a “hold hands” rush to the exits, helping civilians and Khaki Tacs flee.

  • Fight & Flight:
    Blows traded: Manifester stuns Master of the Hunt; Templar wounds Dr. Fervid (revealing a more diabolical form).
    Leandra (invisible) zaps a Banshee (“You’re welcome, dear.”)
    Hannah and The Banshees escape through a glowing banner.
    Dr. Fervid sings “We’ll Meet Again,” blasts Automatic Man, then also jumps through the banner.
    Automatic Man decks Master of the Hunt, killing him.
    The banner remains glowing as the scene ends.

Heroes

  • Mantis Shrimp / Claude Crevette – Temporarily depowered; later engages in the club fight and backs up Leandra.
  • Templar – Intel-gathering among KTs; decisive shots to reduce the band’s control; engages villains.
  • Manifester – Rebrands The Master as “Dave,” orchestrates disguises, mutes the Banshees’ song, and coordinates evac.
  • Automatic Man – Protects civilians (Vicki), trades fire with Dr. Fervid, and finishes Master of the Hunt.

Villains and Threats

  • The Master (“Dave”) – Cooperative under duress; performs the Vicki fix; manipulative but fearful.
  • Dr. Fervid – Band leader with eye-beams and reality-warping songcraft; escapes via banner.
  • Hannah the Hammer – Drummer/dulcimer; leaps into the banner.
  • The Banshees – Sonic enchanters with knives; two throw at Manifester; all flee through the banner.
  • Master of the Hunt – Guitar/axe berserker; killed by Automatic Man 2.0.
  • M.O.L.E.S. – Breach the club to “rescue” the Master; presence heightens chaos.

Other Characters

  • Maxwell Manning – Outraged bandleader, helps lead the crowd to safety.
  • Vicki – Reporter whose false memory is removed; escorted by AM to Dianne.
  • Off-duty Khaki Tacs – Armed crowd; briefly puppeted by the band’s music.

Notes

  • Deal struck: release “Dave” after Vicki is restored and he delivers a full dossier on The Gnome.
  • KT chatter: supersoldier serum and internal comms program underway.
  • The glowing banner functions as a portal; several band members escaped through it.
  • Leandra operates covertly; tech glasses noted.

Return to hotel room; Master still there and restrained but now looks like different now—looks like actor William Smith instead of weird bulbous headed person.

Dave admits there was no agreement, but he’s hoping we’ll release him. Leans on his theme of persecution. We agree to release him if he does his job, but also gives us a writeup of everything he knows about The Gnome.
Manifester creates illusion to disguise them, but accidentally makes them look basically exactly like Macho Man Randy Savage

For now, we have Sammy Von Newman.” Guy looks like a smaller version of Automatic Man. Then they break into a version of Bad Medicine

Manifester and Dave talking: Dave admits it will take 2-3 minutes and will cause her some significant trauma.
