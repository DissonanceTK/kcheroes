Session 23 - Kara Kadaver’s Carnival of Confounding Curiosities

Session Summary

In-Game Date: Fall, 1949
Dimension: 45

Key Events

  • Setting the Board:
    Blurry seeks independence (and cash) and lands work as a courier. Powered people exist here but are rarer than the groups home dimension and tend to be tech- or magic-based.

  • Ballistics Report:
    Shell casings from the Bartle rooftop shootout test as 7.7 mm — a caliber used in various machine guns and notably on Japanese Zero aircraft, tying the incident to the ongoing Axis incursions.

  • No-Load-In Anomaly:
    Manifester watches the venue for Kara Kadaver and notices no gear trucks, unusual for a show this size, despite heavy advertising. The team decides to scout the building.

  • Empty House:
    Mantis Shrimp infiltrates: the main hall is utterly empty—no rigging, sets, or staging. He snags Cracker Jack from concessions on the way out; a janitor witnesses the sliding box and simply shakes his head.

  • Clubhouse Arrangements:
    At the Clubhouse, Al Capone agrees to handle tickets for Manifester and Blurry and places Mantis on security detail (protect the bosses first). Pendergast is in the mix; Ellie Rothschild is furiously working in back. Capone stakes Blurry $50 for proper attire. (Cue shopping montage.)

  • Doors Open… to Elsewhere:
    Tickets read “Admit One” with no seating. Searchlights rake the sky while military spotters scan for aircraft. When the crowd enters, the lobby gives way to a deep, starlit wood and a full CARNIVAL: rides, midway, freak show, hall of mirrors, and a grating clown named Chuckles. The place smells of sawdust and ozone, and it feels real. Mantis posits a portal threshold—far stronger magic than Manifester’s illusions.

  • Hall of Mirrors (Rules You Weren’t Told):
    A fae-like girl warns, “One turn only.” Inside, each hero faces disturbing reflections:

    • Mantis sees his U24 self and a Man in Black who hisses, “You shouldn’t be here.” Mirror-writing answers “Help me.”
    • Blurry phases into mirror-glass like syrup; a hand tries to grab his wrist. A voice: “I watch, you shouldn’t be here.”
    • Manifester’s watch flips wrists; a voice purrs, “Interesting. One of them crossed willingly.” Another warns, “The mirror has your scent now.”
      When Mantis shoots a mirror, gray liminal space shows through cracked panes. The Man in Black appears before Manifester: “Two inside, no balance. Not how the game is played.” The floor shudders. Manifester blinks past him and exits as carnie hands slam the attraction closed. Many guests flee in tears.

  • Madame Twofold’s Readings:
    In a silent tent rich with incense and metallic tang, Madame Twofold delivers auguries:

    • Mantis: “Your hands build and break. You see colors others can’t—a warning, not a gift.”
    • Blurry: “You cross thresholds too easily. The last one took your shadow; the next might take your name.” She later grips his hand: “Bring the girl with the glow. Your other self knows the way.”
    • Manifester: “A cracked mirror can’t reflect truth, only choices. The mirror follows your scent—don’t let it find you asleep.”
      She mutters of spiders in the web, warns “You left one behind—she has the key,” and notes “The Core sings even here—You’ll need her.”

  • Main Tent — The Show Is Real:

    • Kara Kadaver welcomes “my people,” then introduces acts:
    • Bruno the Barbarian Beast (strongman).
    • The Infinite Songbird — a man in a floating glass prism, attended by three veiled crones; within, he phases from man to black panther, then a symphony screams.
    • Kara Kadaver juggles knives as her upper and lower torso separate and rejoin. Manifester confirms: this is true magic, not illusion.

  • Backstage Confrontation — Freeing the Songbird:
    The team trails Bruno and the crones to the freak tent. Inside, the glass prism floats while the witches hum a binding cantus.

    • Manifester’s mass-illusion catches in his throat.
    • Blurry phases the prism on its harmonic;
    • Mantis draws his gun—vibrant shock bursts out (1 dmg to Mantis & Blurry).
    • Bruno decks Mantis (3 dmg), but Manifester blinds him.
    • Blurry’s second phase shatters the prism; the man drops free.
    • A brawl erupts: Mantis batters a crone; Manifester mind-blasts Bruno; Blurry kills another with a single crunching punch.
      The freed man awakens his power—Dissonance—and hurls a witch into a wall.

  • Kara’s Ultimatum:
    As Bruno bolts and the last crone reels, a spectral Kara phases through the canvas: “STOP! You will not ruin the show. If it burns, so do I.” With a gesture, the final witch is yanked into nothingness.
    Kara coolly explains the business arrangement: she tolerated the witches to finish a spell, but they lacked a final component: “your little friend with the otherworldly power source.”
    She lets the team and Dissonance leave—“Next time you come to my carnival, bring the girl.” She tosses a black bone skeleton key at their feet: “When the veil thins, you’ll know when to use it.”

  • Life-Tax:
    Blurry senses patrons’ vitality is lighter on exit than entry—something skimmed a fraction of life-force from every guest.

Heroes

  • Manifester — Scouts the venue; detects true magic; navigates/escapes the Mirror domain; blinds Bruno; anchors the group through the freak-tent melee.
  • Mantis Shrimp — Security detail for Capone; recon of the empty hall; theorizes portal; smashes mirrors; trades blows with Bruno and the crones.
  • Automatic Man — Present but low-profile amid the crowd-control and reconnaissance (no direct confrontation detailed this session).
  • Blurry — Funds plan with courier work; phases the prism to free Dissonance; spots the life-force skim; receives Twofold’s directive: bring “the girl with the glow.”
  • Dissonance — Freed captive (“infinite songbird”); vows that if they ever meet Kara again, it won’t be on her terms.

Villains and Threats

  • Kara Kadaver — Carnival mistress; performs true magic; exerts spectral authority; demands the team bring the “girl with the glow.”
  • The Man in Black — Enforcer of mirror “rules”; warns of balance; seems to hunt Manifester’s “scent.”
  • The Three Witches — Crone-triad binding Dissonance; two killed in the fight; last one banished by Kara.
  • Bruno — Strongman; blindsided (literally) and flees.

Other Characters

  • Al Capone — Provides tickets, bankrolls attire, assigns Mantis to security.
  • Pendergast — Influence felt in arrangements; motives remain suspect.
  • Ellie Rothschild — Working feverishly at the Clubhouse; Twofold’s visions imply Ellie (and the core) still matter here.
  • Chuckles — Obnoxious clown fixture.
  • Madame Twofold — Seer who marks each hero with bespoke omens; delivers the “girl with the glow” instruction.

Notes

  • The Hall of Mirrors appears to be a paradimensional membrane with etiquette (“one in/one out”) enforced by the Man in Black; violations cause seismic backlash.
  • The black bone key likely interacts with thinning veils—keep at the ready near liminal thresholds or eclipses of power.
  • “Girl with the glow” and “you left one behind; she has the key” echo Session 22’s fortune—likely linking back to a known ally touched by the Demon Core or otherworldly power.
  • Life-force micro-drain suggests the carnival finances itself with vitality trickles—not immediately lethal but scalable at mass attendance.
  • Ballistics (7.7 mm) keep the Axis thread alive in D45; watch for Zero flyovers during major events.
