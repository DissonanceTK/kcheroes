Session 30 - Oak Tower

Session Summary

In-Game Date: Fall, 1949
Dimension: 45

Key Events

  • Approach & Cover Story:  
    Acting on an address from The Architect, the team targets the Oak Tower construction site (27th–28th floors under build-out by Donn Rite Construction). Manifester bluffs past a foreman and elevator operator, posing as “war-effort inspectors,” then portals the team onto the 27th floor.

  • Barracks on 27:  
    The open floor is a makeshift workshop/barracks with ~15 off-duty Japanese soldiers arming up. One stands ready with a grenade; several have rifles; benches and workstations suggest Architect-grade fabrication.

  • Crowd Control by Illusion:  
    Manifester unleashes spectacle—brass fanfare, fireworks, and phantom shuriken—dropping five soldiers. A second illusion of liquid fire incapacitates most of the rest, leaving only Grenade-man and one rifleman active.

  • “Pineapple” Fumble:  
    Mantis Shrimp yoinks the grenade (leaving Grenade-man holding the pin), then later hurls it at a jetpack ace outside—only for it to carom off a window mullion and detonate back inside. Grenade-man goes down; Mantis is blasted into a pillar; one rifleman remains.

  • Kidnapper Identified:  
    The jet trooper outside is the same fighter ace who abducted Dissonance. Dissonance answers with a bone-rattling sonic strike, wrecking the jetpack and sending the ace (Reisen) plummeting. Dissonance follows him down to confirm the kill.

  • “Face the Master”:  
    Inside, a samurai with a smoke-wisping magic blade (Shōgun) stalks Manifester among the columns. Manifester’s new reflex-blink triggers on a hit, yeeting him across the room (costing stun but avoiding the cut).

  • Dueling Above Downtown:  
    Mantis zips out/in at wasp size trading blows with Shōgun; cuts land (on Mantis) and near-misses pile up (on the samurai). Manifester lashes Shōgun with a phantom whip, forcing a desperate throw that pins the blade in a wall.

  • Skyfire Returns:  
    As Dissonance arcs back up, Nehan (Rising Sun) arrives. Their blasts cross—the sonic wave staggers her; her radiant beam blows a huge bite out of the 27th floor.

  • The Fall:  
    Manifester’s silverback illusion spooks the samurai into hurling himself out the newly-opened façade. Mantis pursues mid-air; the samurai slows descent by carving the building face, survives the impact, springs up, and sprints for re-entry.

  • Cliffhanger:  
    Manifester creeps the stairwell toward 28; Dissonance stabilizes mid-air; Mantis (battered) hovers at the edge; Shōgun is on the run below—and the Architect remains unlocated.

Heroes

  • Manifester — Social entry; mass crowd-control illusions; reflex-blink defense; silverback feint that sent the samurai out the window; stairwell scout.  
  • Mantis Shrimp — Grenade snatch (and bounce…); aerial dogfight; repeated size-shift melee with the samurai.  
  • Dissonance — Sonic takedown of Reisen; aerial duel with Nehan; battle-space control.  
  • Automatic Man — Overshot the LZ in spectacular fashion (“See you next time!”), landing out in Merriam, KS.

Villains and Threats

  • Japanese Soldiers (≈15) — Most illusion-incapacitated; a few fought on.  
  • Reisen — Jetpack ace; downed/killed after sonic strike and fall.  
  • Shōgun (samurai master) — Magic blade; acrobatic, highly evasive; fell street-level, still mobile.  
  • Nehan (Rising Sun) — Radiant flyer; traded blasts with Dissonance; damaged façade.  
  • Donn Rite Construction / Donnellon — Site control; rival to Al Capone.

Other Characters

  • The Architect — Unseen, presumed present/nearby; work floor outfitted for precision fabrication.  
  • Elevator Operator & Foreman — Unwittingly facilitated entry (one tipped a 25¢ coin from…1987).  
  • Grenade-man — Neutralized by his own “pineapple.”

Notes

  • Architect’s shop layout and precision jigs match earlier jetpack anomalies—supports cross-dimensional tech thesis.  
  • Manifester’s AI-trick module (from MM2) wasn’t needed for organics, but illusions again proved decisive at scale.

Session 26

Played on 10/26/2025 

The Japanese Americans are now posing as Vietnamese soldiers.

Address in note we found from the Architect leads to the Oak Tower Building, 27th floor. It’s a 28 story building in downtown KC that is currently under construction. There are cranes. The streets are blocked off. Construction is being done by Donn Rite Construction, owned by a Donnellon, who we know of as an Irish competitor to Capone. Construction is in progress, partially covered by wooden makeshift roof. Sides and some of roof of 28th floor are open. Lower 14 stories are complete, mostly held by Bell Telephone Company.  

AM jumps towards to top floor but overshoots it, shoots off into space. “Seeeeee youuuu nexttttttt tiiiiiiiiiiiiime!” landing in Miriam Kansas.

Manifester says “Something I learned in Vegas is just act like you belong there, and nobody questions you. So let’s just walk in until we meet some resistance, then Captain France can punch them.”

A Donn Rite construction worker stops us. Manifester “Yes, we’re here from the war effort, to make sure you’re using the concrete and pig iron appropriately.” He radios but lets us onto the elevator, and we make our way up. As we’re on the elevator, there’s an operator. Elevator will only take us to level 24. We go to that floor. Manifester tips 25 cents. (Later he might see it’s a 1987 quarter.)

We step into a hallway. There are other elevators. To the right is a stairway. To left is offices. Dissonance phases through to get us into offices. We head towards corner office where there is a ledge, we enter, away from the park and cranes. It’s a nice day. From there, Manifester creates a portal from where we’re standing into a window on the 27th floor. Mantis heads through first. Dissonance follows, and Manifester comes last.

We enter 27th floor.

INITIATIVE: Manifester, Mantis, Dissonance,

Step through portal just inside a window into what is mostly a large open room being used as combination workshop and living quarter/military barracks. 15 Japanese soldiers (out of uniform) begin scrambling around and grabbing weapons. We don’t see the architect but see workstations that would be compatible with his work. One Japanese man is more prepared, he’s behind a table holding a grenade. At least 3 have rifles. The others are seeking weapons and cover.

Manifester attempts illusion of very loud brass band fanfare heralding our arrival, fireworks outside, and voice saying “You’ve won a free trip back to Japan” then shurikens launching out at them (3 SP). Five of them are “hit” by shuriken; incapacitated. (Not the guy with grenade.) Then Manifester runs to hide behind a column. [There are 4 pillars. He runs to one to the left]

Mantis runs to grenade guy and says “you don’t want to play with this pineapple” and snatches away the grenade, unfortunately leaves man holding the pin. Mantis finishes turn by counting to two.

Dissonance shouts an expletive and phases out to avoid damage. Doesn’t move.

Several soldiers begin firing. Manifester behind pillar is unhit and shouting “You can stop shooting! Don’t shoot the shrimp.” Mantis gets hit (3pts dmg)

We hear someone fly past the building and can see them out the windows. A man with a jetpack more advanced than those we saw in the past. Dissonance recognizes him as his kidnapper.

Manifester again mass illusions that he has a flame thrower and is dousing them all in liquid flames. Succeeds! (13 effort) All but 2 believe they’re on fire and are incapacitated. Grenade man and one other are not affected.

Mantis throws the grenade at the person flying outside, but it hits the window crossbeam and bounces back at him. His eyes go wide, the grenade explodes pushing Mantis back to a pillar. The grenade man collapses on the floor. One soldier left, he’s preparing to shoot Mantis.

Dissonance blasts at the remaining soldier but misses.

Soldier shoots Mantis for 2 dmg.

Jet pack swoops by, shooting machine gun into the floor. Dissonance phases to miss it. The bullets miss Mantis and Manifester. [MANTIS NAT 20]

Manifester hunkers down. Tries to recover but fails.

Mantis flies out window, comes face-to-face with former fighter ace. He has machine gun built into his left arm. Flies past him in effort to punch him; bad guy shoots wildly and hits building across the street.

Dissonance flies out a broken window, sends an sound blast of a thousand people screaming at the fighter ace (15 dmg), blasts him out of the sky. He’s plummeting with a malfunctioning jetpack.

Inside, Manifester hears a metallic door open and someone walking across the room towards him, shows scrunching on broken glass. A voice says “I believe you defeated the student, but now you will face the master.” He hears distinct sound of a sword being drawn.

Manifester rolls recovery, successful. He then pokes around to see a samurai with a blade with wisps of magic coming off it like smoke. Manifester screams for help on the comms “Big guy! Sword! Sword guy in here!”

Mantis shrinks down (2 sp) to wasp size and tries to fly back inside, hits the glass on a window that hadn’t been blown out yet.

Dissonance follows Reisen as he’s falling down to the ground.

The Samurai steps around Manifester’s pillar and swings his sword on him. Manifester takes 2 dmg but not from the sword; teleportation kicks in, sends him flying across the room near a door [He can now use his teleport as a defense, causes 5 stun points]. There’s a Japanese soldier nearby; Manifester makes a face to intimidate him but fails, the soldier instinctively kicks out, hitting him in the nose (1 dmg). Samurai starts doing front and back flips towards Manifester.

Manifester runs out the door. Leads to stairway. Manifester runs down. Uses all movement to get away.

Mantis flies through window, unshrink uppercuts. Samurai dodges Matis with his acrobatics.

Dissonance continues flying towards Reisen to watch him hit the ground.

Samurai slices Mantis for 3 dmg with his magic sword.

Manifester does recovery, heads back up stairs.

Mantis throws a workbench at the Samurai, and he dodges that too.

Dissonance spits on the dead Reisen, then he flies back to the 27th floor.

Samurai swings on Mantis, who dodges.

As he reaches the building, Dissonance sees Neehan (Rising Sun) flying towards the building.

Manifester recovers
